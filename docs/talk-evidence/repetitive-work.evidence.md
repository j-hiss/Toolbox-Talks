**Scope note.** OSHA has no ergonomics standard. FAQ: Senate Joint Resolution 6 "rescinded the original ergonomics rule", and OSHA relies on guidelines and the General Duty Clause, OSH Act Sec. 5(a)(1). All lines are OSHA/NIOSH guidance (kind "guidance"); the General Duty Clause line is stated as the law, worded to match FAQ. The existing `lift` talk covers lifting technique; this talk covers posture, repetition, vibration, tools and task rotation, and does not repeat lifting technique or weight limits. The `hotel-housekeeping` and `patient-handling` talks are other-industry ergonomics and are not duplicated.
