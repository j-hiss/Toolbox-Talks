**Scope note.** OSHA has no standard for snakes, insects, ticks or poisonous plants in general. The only OSHA rule touching the topic is 1926.604(a)(1) (construction site clearing: protection from irritant and toxic plants and first-aid instruction); it is used in the `site-clearing` talk, not here, because this talk covers non-construction industries too. OSHA's landscaping hazards page (osha.gov/landscaping/hazards) has no section on these hazards. All lines are NIOSH guidance. No statistics used (SN has NIOSH figures: 7,000–8,000 bites/yr, about 5 deaths; left out).
