# pipe-handling: evidence

No OSHA standard is specific to pipe handling on drilling rigs. The talk uses OSHA Oil and Gas eTool guidance (kind "guidance"). General standards OSHA lists for struck-by hazards (1910.176, 1910.180, 1910.184, 1910.212) are not quoted as rules here.
Overlap note: oil-struck-by already covers tong swing radius, red zone, rotary table, tong dies/snub lines, hands between stands, and tools up the derrick. This talk is narrowed to the pipe's path: racks, catwalk, V-door, slips, elevators, jerk/spinning chains, stump and stabbing. Swing radius and red zone are not repeated.

Pages fetched: eTool Drilling Ahead; eTool Tripping Out/In; eTool Rigging Up; eTool Casing Operations; OSHA oil and gas hazards page.
Failed: OSHA Fatal Facts 3615 (struck by, pulling casing) and 3616 (struck by, changing bales) both returned provenance errors; not used.

## Hook
- "OSHA says three of every five deaths on oil and gas well sites come from being struck by, caught in, or caught between something." Hazards page: "Three of every five on-site fatalities in the oil and gas extraction industry" are the result of struck-by/caught-in/caught-between hazards (paraphrase)
- "Pipe moves from the racks, up the catwalk, through the V-door and into the slips, and every step has a spot that can catch a hand or a foot." Framing; each step is a section below with eTool pinch/caught hazards (Rigging Up pipe racks "Having feet pinched or crushed"; Drilling Ahead "Being struck by or caught between tubulars"; Tripping "Getting fingers ... pinched between slips"). (paraphrase)

## Racks and catwalk
- "Level the pipe racks." Drilling Ahead, Handling Tubulars: "Level your pipe racks properly." (paraphrase)
- "Chock or pin the tubulars so they can't roll." Drilling Ahead: "Chock or pin tubulars on the racks properly."; hazard "Being struck by rolling or falling tubulars." (paraphrase)
- "Work tubulars from the ends, from ground level." Drilling Ahead: "Work the tubulars from the ends from ground level." (exact sense)
- "Setting up racks and the catwalk can pinch or crush feet." Rigging Up, Pipe Racks and Catwalk: "Having feet pinched or crushed when setting up the pipe racks and catwalk." (paraphrase)
- "Keep a safe distance from equipment that's coming together, and use a tag line to guide racks and catwalks into place." Rigging Up: "Keep a safe distance from equipment that is coming together." "Use a tag line to guide the pipe racks and catwalks into position." (paraphrase)

## Up the V-door
- "Stand clear of suspended, hoisted or moving loads." Drilling Ahead / Casing Operations: "Stand clear of suspended, hoisted, or moving loads." (exact)
- "Watch for pipe and equipment coming up through the V-door." Drilling Ahead: "Be aware of tubulars or equipment being lifted through the V-door." (paraphrase)
- "Hoist slowly to limit pipe momentum, and use a tail rope to guide it as needed." Drilling Ahead, Starting Drilling: "Hoist slowly to limit pipe momentum." "Use tail rope to guide as necessary." (paraphrase)
- "Use a rig floor winch or other powered equipment for heavy casing tools and gear, instead of muscling them by hand." Casing Operations: "Use rig floor winch or other powered equipment to handle heavy casing tools."; Drilling Ahead: "Use mechanical lifting aids such as a rig floor winch." (paraphrase)

## Slips, elevators and chains
- "Slips have three handles." Tripping, Setting Slips: "Slips have three handles and should be lifted jointly by more than one person." (exact sense)
- "Lift them together with more than one person, and keep your hands placed so they can't get caught between the slips and the rotary table." Same, plus hazard "Getting fingers or other body parts pinched between slips or slip handles and rotary table." and "Use proper hand placement when setting slips." (paraphrase)
- "Latch elevators using their handles." Tripping, Latching Elevators: "Ensure workers are instructed in proper latching procedure, including the use of handles on elevators" (paraphrase)
- "An elevator that isn't securely latched can strike you." Tripping hazard: "Being struck by elevators not securely latched." (paraphrase)
- "Never step over a jerk chain, and stay clear of the spinning chain while a connection is being made." Drilling Ahead, Adding Pipe: "Never step over a jerk chain and stay clear of spinning chain when a connection is being made." (exact sense)
- "Use a tail rope on the spinning chain to keep your hands away." Drilling Ahead, Starting Drilling: "Use a tail rope on the spinning chain to keep hands away." (exact sense)

## Hands and feet
- "Keep your hands off the end of the stump and out of the inside of the pipe." Drilling Ahead: "Keep hands away from end of stump or inside of pipe." (paraphrase)
- "Keep your feet and legs out from under the tongs when pipe is being stabbed, and away from the bottom of pipe stands." Drilling Ahead: "Keep feet and legs away from underneath tongs when the pipe is being stabbed." Tripping: "Position feet away from the bottom of the pipe stands." (paraphrase)
- "Keep the area around the rotary table clear of mud, ice, snow and debris that can make you slip or trip." Drilling Ahead: "Keep the work area around the rotary table clean and clear of drilling fluids, mud, ice, snow, debris, and other materials that may cause slipping or tripping." (paraphrase)
