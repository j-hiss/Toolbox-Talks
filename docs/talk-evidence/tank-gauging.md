# tank-gauging evidence

No OSHA standard is written specifically for manual tank gauging. The talk rests on the OSHA/NIOSH Hazard Alert
(02/2016), fetched at https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHA3843.pdf (title confirmed: "Health and
Safety Risks for Workers Involved in Manual Tank Gauging and Sampling at Oil and Gas Extraction Sites"). The alert
states it "is not a standard or regulation" and points to 1910.134 and the General Duty Clause 5(a)(1). The only
standard cited in `sources` is 1910.134, used for the respirator line. The 9-death figure is a NIOSH/OSHA figure from
the alert.
