# frac-silica evidence

Scope check: 1910.1053 (general industry silica) applies to "all occupational exposures to respirable crystalline
silica" except construction, Part 1928 agriculture, and certain sorptive clays, so it covers hydraulic fracturing.
Hydraulic fracturing dates are in **(l)(3)**, not (l)(4): all obligations began June 23, 2018, except engineering
controls under (f)(1) (June 23, 2021) and certain medical surveillance. All dates have passed, so the talk states the
rule as current and does not mention dates.

The 2012 Hazard Alert predates the 2016 silica rule; its "PEL" was the old calculated PEL. The talk says "OSHA's
exposure limit at the time" for the 47% figure. The "10 times the limit" respirator line comes from the alert
(old PEL); it is relative and consistent with a half-face respirator's protection factor, but the current rule's
PEL is 50 µg/m3 (1910.1053(c)). Reviewer: confirm the relative line is acceptable.

Hazard Alert fetched at https://obis.osha.gov/dts/hazardalerts/hydraulic_frac_hazard_alert.html (OSHA host; the
www.osha.gov/dts/... path returned 404) and confirmed against the NIOSH PDF (NIOSH Pub. 2012-166) at
https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/media/pdfs/2026/04/2012-166.pdf. Both carry the same text.
