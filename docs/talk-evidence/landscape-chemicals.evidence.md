**Overlap note.** The library already has `hazcom` (general chemical labels and SDS, all industries incl. land) and `pesticides` (ag, EPA Worker Protection Standard). This talk is narrowed to landscape use: pesticide label rules, mixing/containers/spills, washing up, and a plain statement that the WPS does not cover ornamental landscaping.

**Scope notes.** OSHA has no pesticide-specific or fertilizer-specific standard for landscaping. HazCom (1910.1200) covers SDS and training for hazardous chemicals; (b)(5)(i) exempts FIFRA pesticides from HazCom *labeling* only (SDS and training still apply). Pesticide labels are EPA's. EPA's Worker Protection Standard applies to agricultural plants on agricultural establishments and excepts ornamental landscaping and lawns (170.303(b)); the talk says so. Fertilizers: no fetched page gives fertilizer-specific rules; the talk treats them only through HazCom ("each hazardous chemical") and the label.
