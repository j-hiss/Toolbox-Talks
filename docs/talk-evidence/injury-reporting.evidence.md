# injury-reporting evidence

| English sentence | Source | Supporting quote |
|---|---|---|
| If you get hurt or sick from work, report it. That's your right, and nobody can punish you for it. | OSHA 1904.35(b)(1)(iii)(A)-(B), (b)(1)(iv); OSHA 1904.36 | Hook; paraphrase of "Employees have the right to report work-related injuries and illnesses" and "You must not discharge or in any manner discriminate against any employee for reporting a work-related injury or illness." |
| You have the right to report a work-related injury or illness. | OSHA 1904.35(b)(1)(iii)(A); guidance: osha.gov/workers | exact: "Employees have the right to report work-related injuries and illnesses"; "Report an injury or illness, and get copies of your medical records" |
| Under OSHA's recordkeeping rule, your company has to tell you that right, and tell you how to report. | OSHA 1904.35(a)(1), (b)(1)(ii), (b)(1)(iii) | exact: "You must inform each employee of how he or she is to report a work-related injury or illness to you."; "You must inform each employee of your procedure for reporting"; "You must inform each employee that: (A) Employees have the right to report..." |
| The way to report has to be reasonable. A process that would scare off or discourage a reasonable worker from reporting isn't allowed. | OSHA 1904.35(b)(1)(i) | paraphrase: "You must establish a reasonable procedure for employees to report work-related injuries and illnesses promptly and accurately. A procedure is not reasonable if it would deter or discourage a reasonable employee from accurately reporting" |
| Your company can't fire you or treat you worse for reporting an injury or illness. Section 11(c) of the OSH Act protects you. | OSHA 1904.35(b)(1)(iv); OSHA 1904.36; guidance: OSHA 3812 | exact: "You must not discharge or in any manner discriminate against any employee for reporting"; 1904.36: Section 11(c) "prohibits you from discriminating against an employee for reporting a work-related fatality, injury, or illness"; 3812: "These rights are guaranteed under section 11(c)" |
| Retaliation includes being fired, demoted, disciplined, denied overtime, having your pay or hours cut, or being threatened or harassed. | guidance: OSHA 3812 | exact list items: "Firing or laying off"; "Demoting"; "Denying overtime or promotion"; "Disciplining"; "Reducing pay or hours"; "Intimidation, threats, or harassment" |
| A company policy that discourages injury reporting, like certain incentive programs, can count as retaliation too. | guidance: OSHA 3812 | exact: "Application of an employer policy which discourages injury reporting, such as certain incentive programs" (listed as an adverse action) |
| Report promptly and accurately, using the process your company gave you. | OSHA 1904.35(b)(1)(i)-(ii) | paraphrase: procedure "to report work-related injuries and illnesses promptly and accurately"; employer must "inform each employee of your procedure" |
| Report illnesses too, not just injuries. | OSHA 1904.35(b)(1)(i) | exact: "report work-related injuries and illnesses" |
| You also have the right to review your company's injury and illness records, and to get copies of your own medical records. | OSHA 1904.35(a)(3), (b)(2); guidance: osha.gov/workers | exact: "You must provide access to your injury and illness records for your employees"; workers page: "Review records of work-related injuries and illnesses"; "get copies of your medical records" |
| Some exposures need care right away. If you get blood in a cut or in your eyes, nose or mouth, report it immediately and get medical attention. | guidance: osha.gov/bloodborne-pathogens | paraphrase: if you get blood "in your eyes, nose, mouth, or on broken skin" ... "Report this immediately to your employer and seek immediate medical attention." |
| Reports help find the hazards that would hurt the next person. | guidance: osha.gov/safety-management/hazard-Identification | paraphrase: "By thoroughly investigating incidents and reports, you will identify hazards that are likely to cause future harm." |
