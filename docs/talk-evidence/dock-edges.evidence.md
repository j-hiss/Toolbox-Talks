Corrections to the paragraph numbers given:
- Dockboards are 1910.28(b)(4), not (b)(10). (b)(10) covers outdoor advertising.
- The loading-dock exception is 1910.28(b)(1)(iii), not (b)(1)(ii). (b)(1)(ii) is the residential roofing fall protection plan.
- Ladder use rules are in 1910.23 (added); 1910.29 used only for guardrail height/strength.

Exception stated accurately: it covers only the working side of a loading dock (or loading rack / teeming platform) platform, only when the employer can demonstrate fall protection is infeasible, and only while all three conditions are met. It is not a general dock-edge exemption.

Dropped for lack of a source:
- A specific mezzanine rule: no separate mezzanine paragraph exists; mezzanine edges fall under the general 4-foot rule (1910.28(b)(1)) and are named only as a place that rule covers.
- Dock chains / gates at dock doors: the warehousing page does not address chains. 1910.29(b)(10) allows chains or gates only at hoist areas, and only if equal to guardrails; not used to avoid overstating.
- Fixed ladders over 24 feet (1910.28(b)(9)): accurate but dated phase-in details; left out for length.
- "Three points of contact": not in 1910.23 text; used the sourced "at least one hand".
