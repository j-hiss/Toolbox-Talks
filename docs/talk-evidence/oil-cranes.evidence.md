# oil-cranes: evidence

Applicability: OSHA's oil and gas standards page says all aspects of drilling and servicing except site preparation are covered by 29 CFR 1910, and its hazards page lists 1910.180 (crawler, locomotive and truck cranes) and 1910.184 (slings) for struck-by hazards. 1910.180(b)(1) applies to crawler, locomotive and wheel-mounted cranes (mobile cranes used on location).
Overlap note: "rigging" (con, 1926) and "cranes-hoists" (mfg, overhead cranes 1910.179) exist. This talk is the oil and gas version under 1910.180/1910.184 plus eTool well-site items (catline, V-door, rig-up struck-by).

Pages fetched: 1910.180, 1910.184, eTool Rigging Up, eTool Drilling Ahead, OSHA oil and gas hazards and standards pages.

## Hook
- "During rig-up, OSHA lists being struck by the crane, the load, or a tipping truck as a hazard." eTool Rigging Up hazards: "Being struck by the crane, load, truck, or forklift tipping." (paraphrase)
- "A lift goes right when the operator, the rigging and the crew all do their part." Framing sentence, no factual claim.

## Who runs the crane
- "Only people your company has designated can operate the crane." 1910.180(b)(3): "Only designated personnel shall be permitted to operate a crane covered by this section." (paraphrase)
- "Designated means the employer picked them as qualified." 1910.180(a) definitions: "Designated" means "selected or assigned by the employer or the employer's representative as being qualified" (paraphrase)
- "If that's not you, stay off the controls." Follows from (b)(3).
- "Each crane has a rating chart in the cab where the operator can see it." 1910.180(c)(2): "A substantial and durable rating chart ... shall be provided with each crane ... securely fixed to the crane cab in a location easily visible to the operator" (paraphrase)
- "The crane is never loaded past its rated load." 1910.180(h)(1)(i): "No crane shall be loaded beyond the rated load, except for test purposes" (paraphrase; test exception omitted as not relevant to crews)
- "The operator never leaves the controls while a load is hanging." 1910.180(h)(4)(i): "The operator shall not be permitted to leave his position at the controls while the load is suspended." (paraphrase)

## Check the rigging
- "Each day before use, a competent person your company picks inspects every sling and its fittings." 1910.184(d): "Each day before being used, the sling and all fastenings and attachments shall be inspected" by a competent person designated by the employer (paraphrase)
- "A damaged or defective sling comes out of service right away." 1910.184(d): "Damaged or defective slings shall be immediately removed from service." (exact sense)
- "Before each tour, look over the wire rope and slings, catline ropes and knots, and chains and hooks." eTool Drilling Ahead, Catwalk and V-Door: "Before each tour inspect the: Wire rope and slings, Catline ropes and knots ..., and Chains and hooks." (paraphrase)
- "Don't let a rope lie in standing water." eTool Drilling Ahead: "(do not allow a rope to lie in standing water)" (exact sense)
- "Every sling needs a legible ID marking. No marking, don't use it." 1910.184(c)(14): "Employers must not use slings without affixed and legible identification markings." (paraphrase)
- "Never load a sling past its rated capacity." 1910.184(c)(4): "Slings shall not be loaded in excess of their rated capacities." (paraphrase)
- "Never shorten a sling with a knot or a bolt." 1910.184(c)(2): "Slings shall not be shortened with knots or bolts or other makeshift devices." (paraphrase)
- "No kinked legs, pad sharp edges, and no shock loading." 1910.184(c)(3) "Sling legs shall not be kinked."; (c)(7) "Slings shall be padded or protected from the sharp edges of their loads."; (c)(11) "Shock loading is prohibited." (paraphrase)

## Make the lift
- "Never wrap the hoist rope around the load." 1910.180(h)(2)(i): "The hoist rope shall not be wrapped around the load." (exact sense)
- "Attach the load to the hook with slings or other approved devices." 1910.180(h)(2)(ii): "The load shall be attached to the hook by means of slings or other approved devices." (exact sense)
- "Make sure the load is secure and balanced before it goes up more than a few inches." 1910.180(h)(3)(i)(b): "The load is well secured and properly balanced in the sling or lifting device before it is lifted more than a few inches." (paraphrase)
- "Keep your hands out from between the sling and the load while the sling is being tightened." 1910.184(c)(10): "Hands or fingers shall not be placed between the sling and its load while the sling is being tightened around the load." (paraphrase)
- "Use a tag line when the load could spin in a dangerous way." 1910.180(h)(3)(xvi): "A tag or restraint line shall be used when rotation of the load is hazardous." (paraphrase)
- "Guide it with the line instead of getting under it." eTool Rigging Up (Cellar): "Use a tag line to guide equipment, rather than positioning yourself under suspended loads." (paraphrase)

## Stay clear
- "Everyone stays clear of a load about to be lifted and any load in the air." 1910.184(c)(9): "All employees shall be kept clear of loads about to be lifted and of suspended loads." (paraphrase)
- "Nobody stands or walks under a load on the hook." 1910.180(h)(4)(ii): "No person should be permitted to stand or pass under a load on the hook." (paraphrase; note the standard uses "should")
- "No hoisting, swinging or traveling while anyone is on the load or the hook." 1910.180(h)(3)(v): "No hoisting, lowering, swinging, or traveling shall be done while anyone is on the load or hook." (paraphrase)
- "The operator avoids carrying loads over people." 1910.180(h)(3)(vi): "The operator should avoid carrying loads over people." (exact sense; kept as "avoids", matching "should")
- "Setting up near overhead power lines? Stop and get the clearance rules from your supervisor before the crane moves in." 1910.180(j): "For operations near overhead electric lines, see § 1910.333(c)(3)." (paraphrase; no distance given because 1910.333 was not fetched)

- Discussion question; no factual claim.

## Corrections to hints
- 1910.180 rating chart is (c)(2), not (b). Operator designation is (b)(3). 1910.180 has no swing-radius barricade rule (that is the construction rule 1926.1424), so none is claimed.
