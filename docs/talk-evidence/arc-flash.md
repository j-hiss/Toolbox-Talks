Scope notes for the reviewer:
- 1910.331-.335 (Subpart S work practices) cover general industry. Per 1910.331(c)(1) and its Note 3, qualified-person work on utility generation/transmission/distribution installations is covered by 1910.269 instead. The clothing lines are therefore scoped to "power generation, transmission and distribution work". Construction is covered with 1926.416(a)(1).
- No NFPA 70E values are used. The 2.0 cal/cm² figure is from 1910.269(l)(8)(v) itself, not 70E. The 1.2 cal/cm² arc flash boundary (NFPA 70E, quoted on OSHA 4474) was not used.
- Assignment cited 1910.333(a)(1), (c), 1910.335, 1910.269(l)(8), 1926.416(a)(1): all confirmed. Also used 1910.333(b)(1) (deenergized-but-not-locked = energized).
