# underground-utilities evidence

Fetched 2026-10-07 with WebFetch (quotes capped near 125 chars by the tool):
- 1926.651: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.651
- OSHA 2226 = "Trenching and Excavation Safety", OSHA 2226-10R, 2015: https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/osha2226.pdf
- Interp = OSHA letter of interpretation, Oct. 23, 2003 (reviewed Nov. 22, 2017), hydro-vacuum and non-conductive hand tools under 1926.651(b)(2)-(3): https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/standardinterpretations/2003-10-23 . It replaces a withdrawn July 7, 2003 letter (https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/standardinterpretations/2003-07-07, archived), which was NOT used.
- Topic page https://www.osha.gov/trenching-excavation loaded; it does not mention utilities or 811.

811: mentioned because OSHA 2226 mentions it, framed as OSHA does ("One common industry practice"), not as a federal requirement.
