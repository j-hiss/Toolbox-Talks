# mowers — evidence

Pages fetched 2026-10-07 with WebFetch (summaries plus short exact quotes).
RM = https://www.osha.gov/riding-mowers · LH = https://www.osha.gov/landscaping/hazards · FACE = https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/face/in-house/full201304.html (NIOSH FACE 2013-04) · FIT = https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/181946 (NIOSH "FACE IT: worker safety matters during lawn care", 2021)

**Scope note.** There is no OSHA general-industry or construction standard specific to lawn mowers in the pages fetched (the landscaping hazards page lists mowers only as guidance). Most of the talk is OSHA/NIOSH guidance. Two standards are used: 1910.133(a)(2) (eye protection, general industry) and 1928.57(a)(6)(iii) (agriculture; covers "farm field equipment" = tractors or implements used in agricultural operations; 1928.57 does not mention lawn mowers, so the talk limits that line to farm field equipment). String trimmers: no OSHA/NIOSH page fetched gives trimmer-specific practices beyond RM's "use a string trimmer or push mower" near edges and LH's blade/eye/ear lines; no trimmer-specific claims were written.
