**Scope note.** 1910.269(a)(1)(i)(E): line-clearance tree trimming is covered by 1910.269 — all of it except (r)(1) for qualified employees; for line-clearance tree trimmers who are not qualified employees, paragraphs (a)(2), (a)(3), (b), (c), (g), (k), (p) and (r) apply. (r)(1) intro: "This paragraph does not apply to qualified employees." 1910.269(x) Note 2: under subpart S (1910.331-.335) a line-clearance tree trimmer "is considered to be a 'qualified employee.'" So workers who are neither qualified nor line-clearance tree trimmers fall under 1910.333(c)(3)(i)'s 10-foot rule. OSHA 3861 says it is for operations "not involving line-clearance tree trimming."
