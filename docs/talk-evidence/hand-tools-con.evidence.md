# hand-tools-con evidence

OSHA 3080 = "Hand and Power Tools", https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/osha3080.pdf
Guide = OSHA Construction Focus Four: Caught-In or -Between Hazards instructor guide, https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/caught_iorb_ig.pdf

| English sentence | Source | Supporting quote |
|---|---|---|
| Every tool on this site has to be kept in safe condition, whether the company owns it or you brought it from home. | OSHA 1926.300(a) | paraphrase: "All hand and power tools and similar equipment, whether furnished by the employer or the employee, shall be maintained in a safe condition." |
| Look each tool over for damage before you use it. If it's damaged, don't use it. | guidance: OSHA 3080 | exact: "Examine each tool for damage before use and do not use damaged tools." |
| A damaged power tool comes out of service and gets tagged Do Not Use. | guidance: OSHA 3080 | exact: "Remove all damaged portable electric tools from use and tag them: 'Do Not Use.'" |
| Hand tools count too. | OSHA 1926.301(a) | exact: "Employers shall not issue or permit the use of unsafe hand tools." |
| No wrench with jaws so sprung they slip. | OSHA 1926.301(b) | exact: "Wrenches ... shall not be used when jaws are sprung to the point that slippage occurs." |
| No chisel or wedge with a mushroomed head. | OSHA 1926.301(c) | exact: "Impact tools, such as drift pins, wedges, and chisels, shall be kept free of mushroomed heads." |
| No wooden handle that's cracked, splintered or loose. | OSHA 1926.301(d) | exact: "The wooden handles of tools shall be kept free of splinters or cracks and shall be kept tight in the tool." |
| If a power tool is built to take a guard, the guard is on when you use it. | OSHA 1926.300(b)(1) | exact: "When power operated tools are designed to accommodate guards, they shall be equipped with such guards when in use." |
| A portable circular saw has guards above and below the base plate. | OSHA 1926.304(d) | exact: "All portable, power-driven circular saws shall be equipped with guards above and below the base plate or shoe." |
| When you pull the saw out of the cut, the lower guard has to snap back over the blade right away. | OSHA 1926.304(d) | exact: "When the tool is withdrawn from the work, the lower guard shall automatically and instantly return to the covering position." |
| Never take a guard off while a tool is being used. | guidance: Guide (Focus Four caught-in) | exact: "Never remove a safety guard when a tool is being used." |
| Disconnect the tool before you change a blade, bit or cutter, before you service or clean it, and when you're not using it. | guidance: OSHA 3080 | exact: "Disconnect tools when not using them, before servicing and cleaning them, and when changing accessories such as blades, bits, and cutters." |
| Clamp the work or put it in a vise so both hands are free for the tool. | guidance: OSHA 3080 | exact: "Secure work with clamps or a vise, freeing both hands to operate the tool." |
| Keep people who aren't part of the work at a safe distance. | guidance: OSHA 3080 | exact: "Keep all people not involved with the work at a safe distance from the work area." |
| Point blades and knives away from walkways and from people working near you. | guidance: OSHA 3080 | exact: "should direct the tools away from aisle areas and away from other employees working in close proximity." |
| Never carry a tool by its cord or hose. Never yank the cord or hose to disconnect it. | guidance: OSHA 3080 | exact: "Never carry a tool by the cord or hose." / "Never yank the cord or the hose to disconnect it from the receptacle." |
| Never raise or lower a tool by its cord or hose. | OSHA 1926.302(a)(2), (b)(6) | exact: "The use of electric cords for hoisting or lowering tools shall not be permitted." / "The use of hoses for hoisting or lowering tools shall not be permitted." |
| Keep cords and hoses away from heat, oil and sharp edges. | guidance: OSHA 3080 | exact: "Keep cords and hoses away from heat, oil, and sharp edges." |
| Air tools have to be secured to the hose by a positive means so they can't come apart. | OSHA 1926.302(b)(1) | exact: "Pneumatic power tools shall be secured to the hose or whip by some positive means to prevent the tool from becoming accidentally disconnected."
