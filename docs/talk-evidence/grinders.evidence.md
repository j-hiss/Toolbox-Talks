# grinders evidence

| A cracked grinding wheel can fly apart, and it can happen right at start-up. | guidance: OSHA 3080 (portable abrasive wheel tools) | "If the wheels sound cracked or dead, they must not be used because they could fly apart in operation."; "An abrasive wheel may disintegrate or explode during start-up." (paraphrase) |
| A few quick checks keep the wheel together and keep the pieces off you. | n/a | Framing sentence; the checks themselves are sourced below. |
| The tool rest holds your work. | OSHA 1910.215(a)(4) | "On offhand grinding machines, work rests shall be used to support the work." (paraphrase) |
| Keep it close to the wheel, with a gap of 1/8 inch or less. | OSHA 1910.215(a)(4) | "Work rests shall be kept adjusted closely to the wheel with a maximum opening of one-eighth inch" (exact) |
| A bigger gap lets the work jam between the wheel and the rest, and that can break the wheel. | OSHA 1910.215(a)(4) | "to prevent the work from being jammed between the wheel and the rest, which may cause wheel breakage." (paraphrase) |
| Never adjust the rest while the wheel is turning. | OSHA 1910.215(a)(4) | "The adjustment shall not be made with the wheel in motion." (exact) |
| Clamp it tight after every adjustment. | OSHA 1910.215(a)(4) | "The work rest shall be securely clamped after each adjustment." (exact) |
| Now look at the tongue guard at the top of the opening. The gap between it and the wheel can never be more than 1/4 inch. | OSHA 1910.215(b)(9) | "the distance between the wheel periphery and the adjustable tongue or the end of the peripheral member at the top shall never exceed one-fourth inch." (exact). Scope: guards for bench and floor stands (b)(3) and cylindrical grinders (b)(4) where the operator stands in front of the opening. |
| As the wheel wears down, move the tongue guard in. | OSHA 1910.215(b)(9) | "shall be constructed so that the peripheral protecting member can be adjusted to the constantly decreasing diameter of the wheel." (paraphrase) |
| Look the wheel over closely. | OSHA 1910.215(d)(1); 1910.243(c)(5)(i) | "Immediately before mounting, all wheels shall be closely inspected and sounded by the user (ring test)" (exact) |
| Then ring test it: make sure it's dry and clean, and tap it gently with something light that isn't metal. | OSHA 1910.215(d)(1), (d)(1)(i) | "Wheels should be tapped gently with a light nonmetallic implement"; "Wheels must be dry and free from sawdust when applying the ring test" (paraphrase) |
| A good wheel gives a clear ring. If it sounds dead or cracked, don't use it. | OSHA 1910.215(d)(1), (d)(1)(ii) | "A sound and undamaged wheel will give a clear metallic tone."; "If they sound cracked (dead), they shall not be used." (paraphrase) |
| Check the speed. The machine's spindle speed can't be more than the maximum speed marked on the wheel. | OSHA 1910.215(d)(1); 1910.243(c)(5)(i) | "The spindle speed of the machine shall be checked before mounting of the wheel to be certain that it does not exceed the maximum operating speed marked on the wheel." (exact) |
| The wheel has to fit freely on the spindle. | OSHA 1910.215(d)(2); 1910.243(c)(5)(ii) | "Grinding wheels shall fit freely on the spindle and remain free under all grinding conditions." (exact) |
| Portable grinding wheels need a guard, apart from a few small or special wheels. | OSHA 1910.243(c)(1), (c)(1)(i) | "Abrasive wheels shall be used only on machine provided with safety guards" (exact); exceptions in (c)(1)(i): internal work, mounted wheels 2 inches or smaller, certain cone/plug/pot-ball types. |
| On a right-angle grinder, the guard goes between you and the wheel, set so pieces of a broken wheel get thrown away from you. | OSHA 1910.243(c)(3) | Guard located between operator and wheel; "Adjustment of guard shall be such that pieces of an accidentally broken wheel will be deflected away from the operator." (exact for second part) |
| Let the grinder come up to full speed before you touch the work. | guidance: OSHA 3080 | "Allow the tool to come up to operating speed prior to grinding or cutting." (paraphrase) |
| Don't stand in line with the wheel while it speeds up. | guidance: OSHA 3080 | "The employee should never stand in the plane of rotation of the wheel as it accelerates to full operating speed." (paraphrase) |
| Turn it off when you set it down. | guidance: OSHA 3080 | "Turn off the power when not in use." (paraphrase) |
| Never clamp a hand-held grinder in a vise. | guidance: OSHA 3080 | "Never clamp a hand-held grinder in a vise." (exact) |
| Grinding throws particles. | guidance: OSHA 3080 | "they may throw off flying fragments." (paraphrase) |
| Your company has to make sure you wear eye protection with side protection whenever things can fly. | OSHA 1910.133(a)(1)-(2) | "The employer shall ensure that each affected employee uses eye protection that provides side protection when there is a hazard from flying objects." (paraphrase) |
| With any powered grinder, always wear eye or face protection. | guidance: OSHA 3080 | "When using a powered grinder: Always use eye or face protection." (paraphrase) |
