# radiation evidence

Standard: 29 CFR 1910.1096 fetched from osha.gov. Agency split and practical controls from osha.gov/ionizing-radiation/standards and /control-prevention. Important scope note, stated in the talk: OSHA's standards page says NRC regulates radioactive materials and NRC Agreement States regulate them within their borders; OSHA covers sources NRC does not (e.g. X-ray equipment). 1910.1096(p)(1) and (p)(3) deem NRC licensees and Agreement State licensees/registrants (23 states listed, including Florida) to be "in compliance" with the section for those activities, and (i)(1) points NRC licensees to 10 CFR part 20. So for many hospitals the state or NRC program governs; the talk keeps to principles shared by all and does not quote the OSHA dose table.
