Pages fetched 2026-10-08 (WebFetch):

- K = Kentucky FACE Report 14KY021 (Kentucky FACE program, funded by CDC/NIOSH grant), https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/face/pdfs/14ky021.pdf

Correction / finding: the OSHA trucking loading-unloading page has NO tank-truck guidance on bonding, overfill or falls; it only says tank loading is one of the most hazardous operations. OSHA's general fall protection rules: 1910.21(a) covers all general industry walking-working surfaces, but OSHA interpretation 1996-10-18 (https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/standardinterpretations/1996-10-18) says falls from rolling stock are not cited under Subpart D (General Duty Clause / 1910.132(d) used instead, where feasible). The current status was not fully confirmed (1910.28 page failed to load), so the talk cites NO OSHA fall rule for tank tops; the falls section uses the NIOSH-funded FACE report only. Library check: `fueling` (pump fueling, gas cans), `tank-gauging` (thief-hatch vapors), `trailer-falls` and `oil-fires` exist; this talk is limited to rack loading/unloading: bonding, switch loading, fill valve, tank-top falls.
