# mold evidence

Scope: OSHA has no mold-specific standard. SHIB 03-10-10 (updated 11-08-13): "Currently, there are no federal standards or recommendations, (e.g., OSHA, NIOSH, EPA) for airborne concentrations" of mold, and "This Safety and Health Information Bulletin is not a standard or regulation, and it creates no new legal obligations." The SHIB notes the General Duty Clause still applies: "employers must provide their employees with a workplace free from recognized hazards likely to cause death or serious physical harm." osha.gov/mold/standards lists only sanitation standards (1910.141, 1926.51) and shipyard rules as related. All cleanup guidance below is from the SHIB (kind "guidance"); size levels are the SHIB's recommendations, not rules. 1910.134 is cited only because the SHIB says respirators "must be used in accordance with" it. No EPA material is used as a source (the SHIB references EPA's "Mold Remediation in Schools and Commercial Buildings"; not cited).
