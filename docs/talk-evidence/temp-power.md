Corrections to the assignment:
- "Cords not through doorways" is not the rule. 1926.405(a)(2)(ii)(I) **allows** cords through doorways or pinch points if they are protected. The talk says that.
- "Overhead clearance" is not in 1926.405(a)(2). The heights are in 1926.404(c)(1)(ii) (outdoor open conductors, 600 V or less). Added as a source.
- 1926.405(b)(2) is covers and canopies (confirmed); also used (b)(1) (openings closed).
- 1926.403(i) confirmed: (i)(2)(i) guarding methods for 50 V or more; (i)(2)(iii) warning signs.
