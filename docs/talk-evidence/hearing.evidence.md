Corrections to the paragraph numbers / framing given:
- The instruction to avoid off-the-job noise before the baseline test is 1910.95(g)(5)(iv), not (g)(5)(iii). (g)(5)(iii) is the 14 hours without workplace noise.
- "Action level" is defined in 1910.95(c)(2); (c)(1) is the program trigger.
- There is no standalone "90 dBA = must wear" rule in 1910.95(i)(2). The wear requirement is tied to (b)(1) (over Table G-16 limits after feasible controls) or to 85 dBA TWA plus no baseline / a threshold shift. Talk states it that way.

Dropped / not used:
- "22 million workers are exposed to potentially damaging noise at work each year" (osha.gov/noise): sourced, left out for length.
- Table G-16 durations (90 dBA 8 hr, 95 dBA 4 hr, 100 dBA 2 hr): accurate, left out to keep the spoken talk simple.
- Engineering controls first: covered only inside the wear-requirement sentence.
