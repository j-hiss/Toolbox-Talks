# healthcare-violence evidence

OSHA has no specific workplace violence standard: "There are currently no specific OSHA standards for workplace violence." (osha.gov/workplace-violence/enforcement). Enforcement rests on the General Duty Clause, OSH Act Sec. 5(a)(1). All sources are kind "guidance". OSHA 3148 itself says: "It is not a standard or regulation, and it neither creates new legal obligations". Page numbers below are OSHA 3148 (2016 edition) as reported by the fetch tool.

Overlap: the existing "workplace-violence" talk (industries ["all"]) covers general risk factors, de-escalation, getting out and reporting. This talk narrows to healthcare: patient flagging, alarms/panic buttons, healthcare risk factors (transporting patients, understaffing, waiting rooms), items usable as weapons, post-incident care.
