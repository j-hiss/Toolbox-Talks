Dropped for lack of a source:
- "Never use compressed air on your clothes or skin" / "never blow off yourself": no fetched OSHA page says this directly. STD 01-13-001 does not address clothing; 3080 covers only pointing at people and dead-ending. Kept the sourced "never point at anyone" and "never dead-end against yourself."
- Air entering the body / embolism claims: not sourced; left out.
- Excess flow valve on hoses over 1/2 inch (OSHA 3080): stated in 3080 as a "must" but it mirrors a construction rule (1926.302(b)(7)); not fetched as a general-industry standard, so left out to avoid overstating for manufacturing.
- "Short wire or positive locking device" on hose-to-tool (3080): same reason (construction-rule wording); kept the general "fastened securely" line instead.

Interpretations used: OSHA Instruction STD 01-13-001 (Program Directive #100-1, Feb. 14, 1972; information date 10/30/1978) and the Dec. 6, 1985 letter (relief device permits source pressure at or above 30 psi if it drops to less than 30 psi when dead-ended; PPE "as called for in the standard").
