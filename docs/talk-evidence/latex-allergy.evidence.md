# latex-allergy evidence

Scope: OSHA has no latex-specific standard. osha.gov/latex-allergy/standards: "Latex allergy is addressed in specific OSHA standards for general industry," listing 1910.138 (hand protection) and 1910.1030, with (d)(3)(iii). 1910.1030 applies only to "occupational exposure to blood or other potentially infectious materials" (1910.1030(a)), so the one employer-duty line is conditioned on blood exposure. Everything else is NIOSH Alert 97-135 and the OSHA Hospitals eTool (kind "guidance"). Food service is in scope: NIOSH 97-135 names "food service workers" and gives "food preparation" as a nonlatex-glove task.
