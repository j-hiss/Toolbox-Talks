# lab-chemicals evidence

Standard: 29 CFR 1910.1450. The osha.gov standardnumber page would not load (fetch permission/provenance error); text was checked on the Cornell LII copy (law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/29/1910.1450), which the check brief allows. Sources keep the osha.gov URL. Appendix A is non-mandatory ("Appendix A of this section is non-mandatory but provides guidance..."), so lines from it are worded as "should"/practice and cited as guidance. The osha.gov 1910.1450AppA URL follows OSHA's appendix URL pattern (1910.1048AppA and 1910.1450AppB confirmed to exist) but was not itself loaded; reviewer please confirm.

Scope: (a)(1) applies to "employers engaged in the laboratory use of hazardous chemicals"; laboratory use means lab-scale work, multiple chemicals/procedures, not part of a production process, with protective lab practices and equipment in common use. The talk says "in a lab" and keeps to that.
