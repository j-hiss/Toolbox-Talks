# pallet-jacks evidence

Std = OSHA 1910.178 (https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.178).
Ramps = OSHA PIT eTool, Ramps and Grades (https://www.osha.gov/etools/powered-industrial-trucks/workplace/ramps-grades).
Grocery = OSHA Grocery Warehousing eTool, Transport Techniques (https://www.osha.gov/etools/grocery-warehousing/transport-techniques).
WH = OSHA Warehousing, Hazards and Solutions (https://www.osha.gov/warehousing/hazards-solutions).

| English sentence | Source | Supporting quote |

| A powered pallet jack is in the same family as a forklift under the federal rules. | 1910.178(a)(1) | exact: "fork trucks, tractors, platform lift trucks, motorized hand trucks, and other specialized industrial trucks" |
| A manual jack still moves a lot of weight. | Grocery | exact: "Many of these loads ... are extremely heavy." / "Employees usually move materials through the warehouse on pallet jacks." |
| Both deserve respect. | n/a | Framing, no factual claim. |
| The federal forklift rule covers motorized hand trucks by name. That includes powered pallet jacks. | 1910.178(a)(1) | exact (as above). "Powered pallet jack" = motorized hand truck; eTool Types lists "Class III: Electric Motor Hand Trucks or Hand/Rider Trucks". |
| Your company has to train you and evaluate you before you run one. | 1910.178(l)(1)(i)-(ii) | exact: "The employer shall ensure that each powered industrial truck operator is competent to operate" / "Prior to permitting an employee to operate a powered industrial truck (except for training purposes)," [the employee shall complete training and evaluation] (paraphrase of remainder) |
| A trainee only runs one under direct supervision, where nobody gets put in danger. | 1910.178(l)(2)(i)(A)-(B) | exact: "Trainees may operate a powered industrial truck only:" ... "Where such operation does not endanger the trainee or other employees."; (A) direct supervision of qualified persons (paraphrase) |
| If you have an accident or a near miss, or someone sees you run it unsafely, you get refresher training. | 1910.178(l)(4)(ii)(A)-(B) | exact: "The operator has been observed to operate the vehicle in an unsafe manner;" / "The operator has been involved in an accident or near-miss incident;" |
| Every operator gets evaluated at least once every three years. | 1910.178(l)(4)(iii) | exact: "An evaluation of each powered industrial truck operator's performance shall be conducted at least once every three years." |
| A powered jack gets examined before it's put to work, at least once a day. | 1910.178(q)(7) | exact: "Industrial trucks shall be examined before being placed in service," / "Such examination shall be made at least daily." |
| If it's damaged, needs repair, or is unsafe in any way, it comes out of service until it's fixed. | 1910.178(p)(1) | exact: "If at any time a powered industrial truck is found to be in need of repair, defective, or in any way unsafe," / "the truck shall be taken out of service until it has been restored to safe operating condition." |
| Tell your supervisor. | Grocery | paraphrase: "report problems or difficulties with the machine before the problems lead to injury." |
| Manual jacks wear out too. Flat spots on the wheels make loads unstable and steering hard. | Grocery | exact: "Over long periods of time the solid rubber wheels of pallet jacks and forklifts can develop flat spots" / "resulting in unstable loads and poor handling." / "difficulty in steering and stopping." |
| Report hard steering or stopping before it leads to an injury. | Grocery | exact: "This will allow them to report problems or difficulties with the machine before the problems lead to injury." |
| On a powered jack, only move loads that are stable and within the rated capacity. | 1910.178(o)(1)-(o)(2) | exact: "Only stable or safely arranged loads shall be handled." / "Only loads within the rated capacity of the truck shall be handled." |
| Slow down and sound the horn at cross aisles and blind spots. | 1910.178(n)(4) | exact: "The driver shall be required to slow down and sound the horn at cross aisles and other locations where vision is obstructed." |
| Slow down on wet or slippery floors. | 1910.178(n)(10) | exact: "The driver shall be required to slow down for wet and slippery floors." |
| No riders. People who aren't authorized are not allowed to ride on a powered truck. | 1910.178(m)(3) | exact: "Unauthorized personnel shall not be permitted to ride on powered industrial trucks." |
| Take ramps slowly. | 1910.178(n)(7) | exact: "Grades shall be ascended or descended slowly." |
| Never turn on a ramp. Make your turn before you start up or down. | Ramps | exact: "Never turn on a ramp or incline. Turn prior to the ramp or incline to place forks in proper direction." |
| When you walk a pallet truck on a ramp, loaded or empty, keep the forks pointed downhill, no matter which way you're going. | Ramps | exact: "When walking with a pallet truck with or without a load," / "the forks should be pointed downgrade, regardless of direction of travel." |
| Going into an elevator or other tight space with a powered jack, go in load end first. | 1910.178(n)(13) | exact: "Motorized hand trucks must enter elevator or other confined areas with load end forward." |
