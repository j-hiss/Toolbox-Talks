# anesthetic-gases evidence

OSHA has no specific standard. The OSHA Waste Anesthetic Gases page says: "OSHA standards do not specifically address waste anesthetic gases." OSHA's "Anesthetic Gases: Guidelines for Workplace Exposures" says: "These guidelines are not a new standard or regulation, and they create no new legal obligations" and "OSHA has no permissible exposure limits regulating these agents." Every line is therefore guidance (kind "guidance") and worded as practice, not as an OSHA rule. The osha.gov/waste-anesthetic-gases/standards page failed to load (provenance error) and is not cited. NIOSH RELs (nitrous oxide 25 ppm TWA during administration; halogenated agents 2 ppm ceiling) appear on the guidelines page but were left out of the talk to avoid implying an OSHA limit.
