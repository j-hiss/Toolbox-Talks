# material-hoist — evidence

Pages fetched 2026-10-07 with WebFetch (summaries plus short exact quotes; curl to osha.gov blocked).

Corrections to the assignment's paragraph numbers:
- Rated load is in 1926.552(a)(2) (posting of rated load capacities, speeds, warnings), not (a)(1). (a)(1) is "follow the manufacturer's specifications and limitations" (else a competent professional engineer's determination).
- The "No Riders Allowed" notice is (b)(1)(i); the riding prohibition is (b)(1)(ii). There is no (b)(1)(iv).

Ladder hoists: OSHA 3755 does not mention material hoists or ladder hoists. The only OSHA source found is the June 2, 1998 interpretation letter on a ladder-mounted hoisting wheel: the ladder "would not be considered a hoist for the purposes of OSHA's hoisting regulations" (exact), fall protection under 1926.500-503 still applies, used "if used properly and within manufacturer's guidelines" (exact). It does not address motorized ladder conveyors, so the talk says only what the letter says.
