# hotel-housekeeping evidence

No OSHA lodging/hotel-housekeeping eTool was found. OSHA has no specific ergonomics standard (the Ergonomics page lists
only industry guidelines). Ergonomic lines therefore come from the OSHA Hospitals eTool housekeeping page, whose tasks
(beds, mattresses, tubs, carts, mopping) match hotel room cleaning. Reviewer: this is hospital-housekeeping guidance
applied to hotel work; wording kept to the task-level advice only.

eTool = OSHA Hospitals eTool, Housekeeping MSDs (https://www.osha.gov/etools/hospitals/housekeeping/work-related-musculoskeletal-disorders).
Ergo = OSHA Ergonomics (https://www.osha.gov/ergonomics).
3512 = OSHA/NIOSH InfoSheet, Protecting Workers Who Use Cleaning Chemicals (https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHA3512.pdf).
Laundry = OSHA Hospitals eTool, Laundry, Biological Hazards (https://www.osha.gov/etools/hospitals/laundry/biological-hazards).
LOI-1204 = OSHA interpretation 1992-12-04; LOI-0807 = OSHA interpretation 1992-08-07 (Ritz-Carlton letter).

| English sentence | Source | Supporting quote |
|---|---|---|
| Every room means bending, reaching, lifting and pushing. | eTool / Ergo | paraphrase: "lifting heavy items, bending, reaching overhead, pushing and pulling heavy loads" (Ergo) |
| Do it the hard way all day and it can turn into a back or shoulder injury. | Ergo | paraphrase: those exposures and "performing the same or similar tasks repetitively" lead to MSDs |
| Lifting and holding a mattress to tuck in sheets is a known strain on your back. | eTool | exact: "Lifting and holding mattresses to tuck-in bed linens." (listed hazard) |
| Where fitted sheets are used, you don't have to lift the mattress to change the bed. | eTool | exact: "Use fitted sheets to eliminate lifting mattresses when changing the bed sheets." |
| If something is heavy, lighten the load or get help. Don't muscle it alone. | eTool | exact: "Lighten a load that needs to be lifted or get help when lifting." |
| Speak up early about aches and pains. Reporting early can keep them from becoming a serious injury. | Ergo | paraphrase: "the importance of reporting early symptoms of MSDs"; early reporting can prevent progression to serious injury |
| Use long-handled tools or a step stool instead of reaching overhead or kneeling in the tub. | eTool | exact: "use tools with extended handles, or step stools, or ladders to avoid or limit overhead reaching and kneeling." |
| When you do kneel, wear knee pads. | eTool | exact: "Use knee pads when kneeling." |
| Avoid bending and twisting at the same time. | eTool | exact: "Avoid awkward postures such as bending, twisting, shoulder abduction, and wrist deviation" |
| Change up how you mop so the same muscles don't do all the work. | eTool | exact: "Frequently change mopping styles when mopping (e.g., push/pull and rocking side to side)" |
| Push your cart instead of pulling it, and hold the handles. Grabbing the sides can smash your fingers. | eTool | exact: "Push rather than pull whenever possible." / "Use handles – and not the sides of carts" "to prevent the accidental smashing of hands and fingers." |
| Never mix cleaning products. Bleach and ammonia together can cause severe lung damage or death. | 3512 | exact: "Never mix different cleaning chemicals together." / "Mixing cleaning products that contain bleach and ammonia can cause severe lung damage or death." |
| Wear the gloves and goggles you're given when the job calls for them. | 3512 | paraphrase: employers review PPE "such as gloves and goggles" and provide it |
| Wash your hands with water, not cleaning chemicals, before you eat or drink. | 3512 | paraphrase: workers should wash hands with water, not cleaning chemicals, before eating, drinking, or smoking |
| Needles can hide in linens and trash. | Laundry | exact: contaminated laundry "may contain sharps"; "possible exposure to sharps". Trash: eTool lists garbage handling alongside laundry; reviewer may trim "and trash" if strict. |
| Don't squeeze bags or hold them against your body. | Laundry | paraphrase: avoid holding bags close to the body and do not squeeze them, to prevent "punctures from potential syringes/sharps in the bag." |
| If you find a needle or linens with visible blood, stop and tell your supervisor. | LOI-0807 | paraphrase: employer "may designate specific employees to perform the tasks and procedures" with exposure and train others to hand those off; linens "that did contain visible blood" = exposure |
| Your company decides who handles those items. | LOI-0807 | exact: "the employer's responsibility to determine which job classifications" have exposure |
| Anyone whose job includes handling contaminated sharps gets the protections of the bloodborne pathogens rule. | LOI-1204 | paraphrase: hotel example: an employee who will "come into contact with contaminated sharps" must get the standard's protections; LOI also: OSHA "does not generally consider housekeeping staff in non-health care facilities to have occupational exposure" (so line is scoped to those whose job includes it). 1910.1030(a): "This section applies to all occupational exposure to blood or other potentially infectious materials"; (b) occupational exposure = "reasonably anticipated ... contact with blood" "that may result from the performance of an employee's duties." |
