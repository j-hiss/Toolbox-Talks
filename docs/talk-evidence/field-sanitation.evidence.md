# Evidence: field-sanitation

Standard fetched 2026-10-07 from osha.gov: 1928.110 (https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1928/1928.110). No guidance pages used.

## Hook
- "Heat, germs, holding your urine, and farm chemical residue all wear you down in the field. Clean water, a toilet, and a place to wash your hands are how you fight back."
  - 1928.110(c)(4) (paraphrase): employer must inform employees of hygiene practices "to minimize exposure to the hazards in the field of heat, communicable diseases, retention of urine and agrichemical residues". "Germs" = communicable diseases; "wear you down" is plain-language framing.

## When this rule applies
- "This federal rule covers farms where 11 or more workers do hand labor in the field on the same day."
  - 1928.110(a) (exact): "This section shall apply to any agricultural establishment where eleven (11) or more employees are engaged on any given day in hand-labor operations in the field."
  - Reviewer note: scope is 11+ employees in hand labor on any given day, not 11+ employees overall.
- "Hand labor means farm work done by hand or with hand tools."
  - 1928.110(b) Hand-labor operations (exact): "agricultural activities or agricultural operations performed by hand or with hand tools."
- "It does not cover logging, caring for or feeding livestock, or work inside permanent buildings like packing houses."
  - 1928.110(b) (exact): "Hand-labor does not include such activities as logging operations, the care or feeding of livestock, or hand-labor operations in permanent structures (e.g., canning facilities or packing houses)."
- "When it applies, your company provides the water, toilets, and handwashing at no cost to you."
  - 1928.110(c) (exact): "Agricultural employers shall provide the following for employees engaged in hand-labor operations in the field, without cost to the employee:"

## Drinking water
- "Your company has to put clean drinking water where everyone can easily get to it."
  - 1928.110(c)(1)(i) (exact): "Potable water shall be provided and placed in locations readily accessible to all employees." "Clean drinking water" = potable water (defined in (b) as meeting state/local or EPA 40 CFR 141 drinking water standards).
- "It has to be cool enough, and there has to be enough of it for the heat, the humidity, and the work."
  - 1928.110(c)(1)(ii) (paraphrase): "The water shall be suitably cool and in sufficient amounts, taking into account the air temperature, humidity and the nature of the work performed".
- "Water comes in single-use cups or from a fountain. No shared cups and no dippers."
  - 1928.110(c)(1)(iii) (exact): "The water shall be dispensed in single-use drinking cups or by fountains. The use of common drinking cups or dippers is prohibited."
- "Water containers get refilled every day, or more often if needed, and stay covered."
  - 1928.110(c)(3)(i) (exact): "shall be refilled daily or more often as necessary, shall be kept covered and shall be regularly cleaned."

## Toilets and handwashing
- "There has to be one toilet and one handwashing station for every 20 workers, or part of 20."
  - 1928.110(c)(2)(i) (exact): "One toilet facility and one handwashing facility shall be provided for each twenty (20) employees or fraction thereof, except as stated in paragraph (c)(2)(v) of this section."
- "They go close to each other, within a quarter-mile walk of where you are working."
  - 1928.110(c)(2)(iii) (exact): "Toilet and handwashing facilities shall be accessibly located and in close proximity to each other. The facilities shall be located within a one-quarter-mile walk of each hand laborer's place of work in the field."
- "If the land makes that impossible, they go at the closest point a vehicle can reach."
  - 1928.110(c)(2)(iv) (exact): "Where due to terrain it is not feasible to locate facilities as required above, the facilities shall be located at the point of closest vehicular access."
- "A handwashing station has clean water, soap, and single-use towels."
  - 1928.110(b) Handwashing facility (exact): "a basin, container, or outlet with an adequate supply of potable water, soap and single-use towels."
- "A toilet has toilet paper, privacy, and a door that closes on its own and latches from inside."
  - 1928.110(b) Toilet facility (exact): "supplied with toilet paper adequate to employee needs." 1928.110(c)(2)(ii) (exact): "have self-closing doors that can be closed and latched from the inside and shall be constructed to insure privacy."
- "If the whole job that day is 3 hours or less, counting travel to and from the field, toilets and handwashing are not required. Drinking water still is."
  - 1928.110(c)(2)(v) (exact): "Toilet and handwashing facilities are not required for employees who perform field work for a period of three (3) hours or less (including transportation time to and from the field) during the day."
  - Reviewer note: the exception sits in (c)(2) only; (c)(1) drinking water has no such exception, so "Drinking water still is" follows from the structure of the rule.

## Use them
- "Your company has to tell you where the water and toilets are, and give you reasonable chances during the day to use them."
  - 1928.110(c)(4) (exact): "The employer shall notify each employee of the location of the sanitation facilities and water and shall allow each employee reasonable opportunities during the workday to use them."
- "Drink water often, especially on hot days."
  - 1928.110(c)(4)(ii) (exact): "Drink water frequently and especially on hot days;"
- "Urinate as often as you need to. Don't hold it."
  - 1928.110(c)(4)(iii) (exact): "Urinate as frequently as necessary;" plus (c)(4) hazard of "retention of urine".
- "Wash your hands before and after using the toilet, and before you eat or smoke."
  - 1928.110(c)(4)(iv) (exact): "Wash hands both before and after using the toilet; and" (c)(4)(v): "Wash hands before eating and smoking."
- "Toilets have to work and be kept clean, and handwashing water has to be refilled."
  - 1928.110(c)(3)(ii) (exact): "Toilet facilities shall be operational and maintained in clean and sanitary condition." (c)(3)(iii) (exact): "Handwashing facilities shall be refilled with potable water as necessary to ensure an adequate supply".
- "If something runs out or breaks, tell your supervisor."
  - Action prompt, not a factual claim; supports the employer's (c)(3) maintenance duty.

## Ask
- "From where we're working today, where is the nearest drinking water, toilet, and handwashing station?" — crew question; ties to (c)(4) notification.
