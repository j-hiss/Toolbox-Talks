# grain-bins evidence

Scope note (farm vs commercial):
- 1910.272(b)(1): paragraphs (a)-(n) "apply to grain elevators, feed mills, flour mills, rice mills, dust pelletizing plants, dry corn mills, soybean flaking operations, and the dry grinding operations of soycake."
- OSHA interpretation letter of July 29, 1991: 1910.272 "is applicable to establishments handling bulk raw agricultural commodities" "except for grain handling facilities engaged primarily in the production of crops or live stock," "such as farm or feed lots."
- The OSHA grain-handling page, hazard alert and wallet card do not address farms. The Agricultural Operations hazards page warns of farm-bin engulfment. So the bin-entry steps are presented as a rule for commercial facilities and as OSHA guidance on farms.

Paragraph corrections to the brief's framing: within (g), (g)(1)(i) is the entry permit, (g)(1)(ii) lockout, (g)(1)(iii) atmosphere testing, (g)(1)(iv) walking down grain, (g)(2) harness/lifeline, (g)(3) observer and communications, (g)(4) rescue equipment, (g)(5) observer rescue training, (g)(6) bridging/buildup. Flat storage is the (h) exception.
