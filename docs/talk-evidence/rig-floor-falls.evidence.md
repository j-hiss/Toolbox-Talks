# rig-floor-falls: evidence

Applicability: OSHA's oil and gas standards page: "site preparation is the only aspect of oil and gas well drilling and servicing operations covered by 29 CFR 1926" and "All other aspects ... are covered by 29 CFR 1910". The Nov 12, 2010 interpretation (to IADC) confirms 1910 applies to rig-up and rig-down. So the fall rule cited is 1910.28 (4 feet), not 1926 Subpart M (6 feet), even though some eTool pages still bracket-cite 1926 Subpart M. I did not find a separate OSHA interpretation specifically on derrick fall protection; none is claimed.
Overlap note: oil-struck-by already says "don't carry tools up the derrick ladder, raise them with a line". Kept as one line here because dropped objects are part of working at height; the talk is otherwise about falls.

Pages fetched: 1910.28; osha.gov/oil-and-gas-extraction/standards; standard interpretation 2010-11-12; osha.gov/oil-and-gas-extraction/hazards (Falls section, summary); Fatal Facts 3617 (osha.gov/Publications/OSHA-FF-3617.pdf); eTool Tripping Out/In; eTool Rigging Up; eTool Servicing General Services.

## Hook
- "In one OSHA case, a derrickman climbed back to the board after a break without hooking up his fall protection." FF 3617: "The derrickman climbed back to the derrick board after a break without attaching his fall protection" (paraphrase)
- "He lost his balance and fell 90 feet to the rig floor." FF 3617: "he lost his balance and fell 90 ft. to the rig floor" (paraphrase)

## The rule
- "Drilling and servicing work falls under OSHA's general industry rules." Oil and gas standards page: "All other aspects of oil and gas well drilling and servicing operations are covered by 29 CFR 1910" (paraphrase)
- "Site prep is the exception." Same page: "site preparation is the only aspect ... covered by 29 CFR 1926" (paraphrase)
- "Under those rules, your company has to protect you on any open side or edge 4 feet or more above a lower level." 1910.28(b)(1)(i): "the employer must ensure that each employee on a walking-working surface with an unprotected side or edge that is 4 feet (1.2 m) or more above a lower level is protected from falling" (paraphrase; also (b)(15) for surfaces not otherwise addressed)
- "That means a guardrail, a safety net, or a personal fall protection system." 1910.28(b)(1)(i)(A)-(C): "Guardrail systems; Safety net systems; or Personal fall protection systems, such as personal fall arrest, travel restraint, or positioning systems." (paraphrase)
- "OSHA requires fall protection for falls from the mast, the drilling platform and other elevated equipment." Hazards page, Falls: "OSHA requires fall protection to prevent falls from the mast, drilling platform, and other elevated equipment." (paraphrase)

## Up in the derrick
- "Wear a full body harness and stay 100% tied off while you work in the derrick." eTool Tripping, Monkeyboard: "Wear appropriate fall protection including a full body harness." "Practice 100% tie-off while working in the derrick." (paraphrase)
- "Never unhook your fall arrest system while you're up there." eTool General Services, Pulling and Running Rods: "Never disconnect personal fall arrest systems while working in the derrick." (paraphrase)
- "Use the climb assist on the ladder." eTool Tripping, Monkeyboard: "Use climb assist device." (paraphrase)
- "The derrickman in that case unhooked from the climb assist and never clipped in at the board." FF 3617: after unhooking from the climb assist, he climbed to the board without attaching fall protection (paraphrase)
- "Know how to use the derrick emergency escape device before you ever need it." eTool Tripping: "Train personnel in use of emergency escape device." (paraphrase)

## Ladders, stairs and guardrails
- "Use only ladders in good repair, with no missing rungs." eTool Rigging Up, Stairways: "Use only ladders in good repair that do not have missing rungs." (paraphrase)
- "Stairs with missing or damaged steps get fixed before they go in." eTool Rigging Up: "Do not install stairs with missing or damaged steps. Repair them before installing them." (paraphrase)
- "Guardrails go up before anyone works in an elevated area." eTool Rigging Up: "Place guardrails in place prior to working in elevated areas. [29 CFR 1910.28]" (paraphrase)
- "Never use a guardrail as an anchor, or to lift or hold a load." eTool Rigging Up, Cellar: "Do not use guardrails for anchor points or for lifting or supporting loads." (paraphrase)
- "Wear fall protection while you put guardrails up or take them down." eTool Rigging Up: "Use fall protection when installing or removing guardrails." (paraphrase)

## Dropped objects
- "Tie off every tool you use up high." eTool Tripping: "Implement a dropped objects program, such as tie-off for all tools."; Rigging Up: "Attach safety lines to all tools hanging from the rig." (paraphrase)
- "Don't carry tools up the derrick ladder. Raise them with a line." eTool Tripping: "Do not carry tools while climbing the derrick ladder. Raise tools with a line to any worker above the derrick floor." (paraphrase)
- "Before the derrick goes up, check it for loose tools." eTool Rigging Up: "Check the derrick for unsecured tools before raising it." (paraphrase)
- "When people are working overhead, use extra caution below and wear your hard hat." eTool Tripping: "Use extra caution while personnel are working overhead." 1910.28(c): "When an employee is exposed to falling objects, the employer must ensure that each employee wears head protection" (paraphrase)
- "Keep slip-resistant surfaces in good shape, and keep the floor clear of mud, ice and debris." eTool Tripping: "Use slip-resistant coatings or materials on working surfaces."; eTool Drilling Ahead: "Keep the work area around the rotary table clean and clear of mud, ice, snow, debris..." (paraphrase)

## Ask
- "when did you last inspect your harness and the climb assist?" FF 3617 prevention: "Inspect all fall-related equipment (guardrails, ladders, PFAS) to make sure that they are not damaged or deteriorated." (discussion prompt)

## Dropped
- Fixed-ladder rules in 1910.28(b)(9) (over 24 feet; ladder safety system dates 2018/2036). Accurate but too conditional for a crew talk and rig ladders vary; left out rather than oversimplify.
- 1926 Subpart M 6-foot rule: not applicable to drilling/servicing except site prep.
