# Evidence: labor-camps

Fetched 2026-10-08 via WebFetch: OSHA 1910.142 (https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.142), 1928.21, OSHA Field Operations Manual Ch. 12 (https://www.osha.gov/fom/chapter-12).
Scope: 1928.21(a)(1) applies 1910.142 to agriculture. FOM Ch. 12 (exact): "29 CFR 1910.142, the Temporary Labor Camp standard, is applicable to both agricultural and non-agricultural workplaces." FOM also says agricultural housing whose construction started before April 3, 1980 may follow a separate DOL (ETA) housing standard instead ("Choices shall be limited to Subpart E of 29 CFR Part 654, or §1910.142" as quoted by the fetch; the ETA rule is codified at 20 CFR 654 Subpart E — reviewer should confirm). The talk does not get into that split; the hook keeps scope general ("a federal rule covers that housing"). Reviewer: consider whether to add "some older farm housing follows a different Labor Department rule" if the fact-checker can confirm the citation.
1910.142 has no scope paragraph in the text. FOM: "temporary" refers to a discrete employment period; covered housing is employer-provided housing that is a term or condition of employment.

## Hook
- "When housing comes with the job, a federal rule covers that housing." 1928.21(a)(1); FOM Ch. 12 (paraphrase): required employer-provided housing that is a term or condition of employment.
- "Know what it should have, and speak up when something is wrong." Framing/action prompt.

## Sleeping quarters
- "Each sleeping room needs at least 50 square feet of floor space per person, and a ceiling at least 7 feet high." 1910.142(b)(2) (exact fragments): "at least 50 square feet of floor space for each occupant"; "At least a 7-foot ceiling shall be provided."
- "Beds have to be at least 36 inches apart, side to side and end to end, and at least 12 inches off the floor. Triple-deck bunks are not allowed." 1910.142(b)(3) (exact fragments): "spaced not closer than 36 inches both laterally and end to end"; "elevated at least 12 inches from the floor"; "Triple-deck bunks are prohibited." (Double-deck 48-inch spacing omitted.)
- "Shelters have to protect you from the weather. Outside openings need screens, and screen doors have to close on their own." 1910.142(b)(1) (exact): "Every shelter in the camp shall be constructed in a manner which will provide protection against the elements." (b)(8) (exact): "All exterior openings shall be effectively screened with 16-mesh material." "All screen doors shall be equipped with self-closing devices."
- "Central kitchens and dining halls are kept separate from sleeping rooms." 1910.142(i)(2) (exact): kitchens and dining halls "separate from the sleeping quarters of any of the workers or their families"; "There shall be no direct opening from living or sleeping quarters into a kitchen or dining hall." (i)(1) context: where central dining or multiple-family feeding is provided.

## Water, toilets and washing
- "The camp needs a water supply approved by the health authority, able to deliver 35 gallons per person per day." 1910.142(c)(1) (exact): "An adequate and convenient water supply, approved by the appropriate health authority, shall be provided"; (c)(2) (exact): "capable of delivering 35 gallons per person per day to the campsite."
- "Shared drinking cups are not allowed." 1910.142(c)(4) (exact): "Common drinking cups are prohibited."
- "There has to be a toilet room within 200 feet of the door of each sleeping room." 1910.142(d)(3) (exact): "A toilet room shall be located within 200 feet of the door of each sleeping room."
- "Toilet rooms are lit day and night, stocked with toilet paper, and cleaned at least once a day." 1910.142(d)(8): lit "at all hours of the day and night"; (d)(9): "An adequate supply of toilet paper"; (d)(10) (exact): "They shall be cleaned at least daily."
- "The camp needs a shower for every 10 people, a handwash basin for every 6 people in shared facilities or one per family shelter, and hot and cold running water." 1910.142(f)(1)(i): one handwash basin "per family shelter or per six persons in shared facilities"; (f)(1)(ii): "Shower head for every 10 persons"; (f)(3): "an adequate supply of hot and cold running water." "Shared facilities" qualifier applies only to the basin ratio, as in (f)(1)(i).

## Heat, cooking and garbage
- "This rule doesn't spell out fire extinguishers." FOM Ch. 12 (exact): "the TLC standard has no provisions that specifically apply to fire protection." Verified: 1910.142 text has no mention of extinguishers or exits.
- "It does say heating, cooking and water-heating equipment has to be installed under state and local codes." 1910.142(b)(11) (exact): "All heating, cooking, and water heating equipment shall be installed in accordance with State and local ordinances..."
- "A camp used in cold weather needs adequate heat." 1910.142(b)(11) (exact): "If a camp is used during cold weather, adequate heating equipment shall be provided."
- "If a stove or heater looks unsafe, report it to the person who runs the camp." Action prompt.
- "Each shelter needs a garbage can within 100 feet. Cans get emptied when full, and at least twice a week." 1910.142(h)(1): container "within 100 feet of each shelter on a wooden, metal, or concrete stand"; (h)(3) (exact): "emptied when full, but not less than twice a week."
- "The camp has to take real steps to keep insects and rodents out." 1910.142(j) (exact): "Effective measures shall be taken to prevent infestation by and harborage of animal or insect vectors or pests."

## Getting sick or hurt
- "Every camp has to keep first aid facilities, in the charge of a person trained in first aid." 1910.142(k)(1) (exact): "Adequate first aid facilities approved by a health authority shall be maintained and made available in every labor camp for the emergency treatment of injured persons." (k)(2): "in charge of a person trained to administer first aid."
- "The camp superintendent has to report suspected contagious disease to the local health officer, and suspected food poisoning to the health authority, right away." 1910.142(l)(1): superintendent must "report immediately to the local health officer" any individual with a suspected communicable disease; (l)(2): suspected food poisoning or unusual prevalence of certain illnesses — "report immediately the existence of the outbreak to the health authority."
- "Nobody with a contagious disease can work preparing, cooking or serving food." 1910.142(i)(3) (exact): "No person with any communicable disease shall be employed or permitted to work in the preparation, cooking, serving..."
