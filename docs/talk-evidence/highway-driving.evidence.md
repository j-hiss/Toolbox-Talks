# highway-driving evidence

Agency: these are DOT/FMCSA rules (49 CFR Part 392), not OSHA. Per OSHA's Highway Driving page, DOT preempts OSHA
over interstate trucking on public roads for hazards FMCSA regulations address; OSHA keeps authority over intrastate
trucking. OSHA has no highway-driving standard of its own; the OSHA page is reference/jurisdiction only and gives no
driving guidance. Narrowed from existing "vehicles" (seat belts, phones, drowsy driving, backing, for all
industries) and "fatigue" (general sleep): this talk is over-the-road CMV rules (FMCSA phone, weather, fatigue rules),
following distance in work zones, and a long-haul FACE case. Seat belts and backing are left to "vehicles".

Text of 392.3 and 392.14 read on eCFR (ecfr.gov). Text of 392.80 and 392.82 read on the Cornell LII mirror
(law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/392.80, /392.82); sources cite the eCFR section URLs per the brief.
