# battery-charging evidence

Std = OSHA 1910.178 (https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.178).
eTool-E = OSHA PIT eTool, Power Sources: Electric (https://www.osha.gov/etools/powered-industrial-trucks/types-fundamentals/power-sources/electrical).
eTool-IC = OSHA PIT eTool, Power Sources: Internal Combustion (https://www.osha.gov/etools/powered-industrial-trucks/types-fundamentals/power-sources/internal-combustion).

| English sentence | Source | Supporting quote |
|---|---|---|
| A forklift battery is very heavy, it's full of acid, and it gives off a gas that can explode. | eTool-E | exact: "Batteries are very heavy." / "Batteries contain sulfuric acid that is highly corrosive" / "batteries can give off highly explosive hydrogen fumes." |
| The charging area is not a break room. | n/a | Framing line, no factual claim. |
| Charge batteries only in the area set aside for charging. | 1910.178(g)(1); eTool-E | exact: "Battery charging installations shall be located in areas designated for that purpose." / "Charge batteries in the designated battery charging area." |
| Your company has to set that area up with a way to flush and neutralize spilled acid, fire protection, ventilation, and protection so trucks can't damage the chargers. | 1910.178(g)(2) | exact: "Facilities shall be provided for flushing and neutralizing spilled electrolyte, for fire protection," ... "for protecting charging apparatus from damage by trucks, and for adequate ventilation for dispersal of fumes from gassing batteries." |
| Toward the end of a charge, batteries give off hydrogen gas. It's highly explosive. | eTool-E | exact: "Toward the end of the battery charging process, batteries can give off highly explosive hydrogen fumes." |
| No smoking, no open flames, no sparks in the charging area. | 1910.178(g)(10), (g)(11) | exact: "Smoking shall be prohibited in the charging area." / "Precautions shall be taken to prevent open flames, sparks, or electric arcs in battery charging areas." |
| Only trained people charge and change forklift batteries. | eTool-E | exact: "Only trained personnel should charge and change batteries in electric forklifts." |
| Position the truck and set the brake before you change or charge a battery. | 1910.178(g)(8) | exact: "Trucks shall be properly positioned and brake applied before attempting to change or charge batteries." |
| Open the battery cover so heat can escape, and make sure the vent caps are working. | 1910.178(g)(9) | exact: "Care shall be taken to assure that vent caps are functioning." / eTool-E: "The battery (or compartment) cover(s) shall be open to dissipate heat." |
| Take off metal jewelry. | eTool-E | exact: "Remove all metallic jewelry before recharging." |
| Keep tools and other metal off the top of an uncovered battery. | 1910.178(g)(12) | exact: "Tools and other metallic objects shall be kept away from the top of uncovered batteries." |
| Touching the cells can cause a short that burns your skin. | eTool-E | exact: "Contact with battery cells can cause electrical short circuits, which can burn unprotected skin." |
| Unplug and turn off the charger before you connect or disconnect the clamps. | eTool-E | exact: "Unplug and turn off the charger before connecting or disconnecting the clamp connections." |
| Battery acid is sulfuric acid, and it's highly corrosive. | eTool-E | exact: "Battery acid is dilute sulfuric acid." / "Batteries contain sulfuric acid that is highly corrosive" |
| Wear a face shield, safety goggles, and rubber or neoprene gloves and apron. | eTool-E | exact: "Wear personal protective equipment (face shield, safety goggles, neoprene or rubber gloves and apron)." |
| Acid in your eyes? Flush them at the eyewash with clean water for 15 minutes. | eTool-E | exact: "Remove safety glasses and flush eyes with clean water in eyewash for 15 minutes." |
| Acid on your skin? Take off the soaked clothes and flush the skin for 15 minutes. | eTool-E | exact: "Remove acid soaked clothing immediately." / "Flush acid contacted skin with clean water for 15 minutes." |
| If you handle electrolyte, pour acid into water. Never pour water into acid. | 1910.178(g)(7) | exact: "When charging batteries, acid shall be poured into water; water shall not be poured into acid." |
| Use the hoist, lifting beam, or other lifting equipment provided. | 1910.178(g)(4); eTool-E | exact: "A conveyor, overhead hoist, or equivalent material handling equipment shall be provided for handling batteries." / "Use a lifting beam or equivalent material handling equipment when lifting the battery." |
| Never use a chain with two hooks. | eTool-E | exact: "Do not use a chain with two hooks." |
| Put the battery back in the right position and secure it in the truck. | 1910.178(g)(5) | exact: "Reinstalled batteries shall be properly positioned and secured in the truck." |
| Only trained and authorized people replace propane tanks. | eTool-IC | exact: "Only trained and authorized personnel should replace LPG containers." |
| Propane is extremely flammable, and it can give you frostbite on bare skin. | eTool-IC | exact: "LPG is extremely flammable." / "If your skin is exposed to LPG, you can get frostbite." |
| Propane vapor is heavier than air and collects in low spots. | eTool-IC | exact: "LPG vapor is heavier than air and will seek the lowest lying area." / "it will collect in pockets" |
| Don't refuel in confined areas, | eTool-IC | exact: "Do not refuel LPG-powered trucks in confined areas where LPG vapors could collect if a leak occurs." |
| and turn the service valve off when you park the truck for a long time. | eTool-IC | exact: "When parking LPG-powered trucks for a long period of time, turn the service valve off." |
| Ask: Where are the eyewash and the acid neutralizer for our charging area? | eTool-E | Prompt; basis: "An eyewash able to provide a 15 minute flow." / "Soda ash or other neutralization materials in the immediate area." |

1910.178(f)(2) is cited because LP-gas storage and handling "shall be in accordance with NFPA" No. 58; the standard text itself has no propane-changing steps, so all propane lines come from eTool-IC.

Verified but not used (length): (g)(6) carboy tilter or siphon; eTool-E charger clamp order, 25-ampere sealed-vent limit, 1 lb baking soda per gallon, non-sparking tools, contact-lens wearers use splash goggles.
