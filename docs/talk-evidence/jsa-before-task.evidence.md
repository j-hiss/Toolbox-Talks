# jsa-before-task evidence

Pages fetched 2026-10-08 (WebFetch):
- J = OSHA 3071 Job Hazard Analysis (2002 rev.), https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/osha3071.pdf
- R = OSHA Recommended Practices, Hazard Identification and Assessment, https://www.osha.gov/safety-management/hazard-identification (the capital-I URL in the assignment failed provenance; lowercase URL loaded)

No OSHA standard requires a job safety/hazard analysis for all industries. The talk is guidance only (kind "guidance"); no line claims a rule. Library check: `near-miss` and `emerg` exist; this talk is the step-by-step JSA method, not near-miss reporting.

| A job safety analysis, or JSA, means looking at a job step by step to find the hazards before they hurt someone. | J intro | exact: "A job hazard analysis is a technique that focuses on job tasks as a way to identify hazards before they occur." |
| OSHA calls it a job hazard analysis. | J title | exact: title "Job Hazard Analysis" |
