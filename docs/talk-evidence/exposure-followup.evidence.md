# exposure-followup evidence

Scope: 1910.1030 applies to occupational exposure to blood or other potentially infectious materials (OPIM). The talk says "If OSHA's bloodborne pathogens rule covers your job" before the employer duties, because janitorial/facilities staff are covered only where they have occupational exposure. Overlap: "sharps" covers needles and containers and has a 3-line post-exposure section; "first-aid-blood" has 2 lines. This talk covers only the after-exposure steps in detail.
