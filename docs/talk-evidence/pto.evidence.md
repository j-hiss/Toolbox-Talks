# pto evidence

Paragraph notes: 1928.57(b)(1)(i) is the general PTO-shaft guarding rule; (b)(1)(ii) is the tractor master shield; (b)(1)(iii) covers equipment that requires the master shield removed and guarding of PTO-driven equipment. Farmstead PTO guarding is (c)(1). Operating instructions are (a)(6)(i)-(v). Per (a)(3), some provisions (not (b)(1)) don't apply to equipment built before October 25, 1976; no line in the talk depends on those.
