# airborne-infection evidence

There is no OSHA standard specific to TB. Per OSHA's TB Standards page, TB exposure is addressed through the General Duty Clause (enforcement directive CPL 02-02-078), 1910.134 respiratory protection, 1904.11 recordkeeping, and 1910.1020 records access. The TB Standards page also lists the Federal Register notice "Respiratory Protection for M. Tuberculosis; Final Rule; Revocation", which is why respirators for TB fall under 1910.134 (checked, as the assignment asked). Practical lines rely on OSHA TB guidance pages (kind "guidance").
