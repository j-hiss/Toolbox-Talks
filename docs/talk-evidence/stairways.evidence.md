| 1.1 | If there's a step up or down of 19 inches or more, and no ramp, runway, sloped embankment, or personnel hoist, your company has to provide a stairway or a ladder. | 1926.1051(a) | exact: "A stairway or ladder shall be provided at all personnel points of access" "where there is a break in elevation of 19 inches (48 cm) or more" "and no ramp, runway, sloped embankment, or personnel hoist is provided." |
| 1.2 | If there's only one way between levels, keep it clear. | 1926.1051(a)(3) | exact: "When a building or structure has only one point of access between levels," "that point of access shall be kept clear to permit free passage of employees." |
| 1.3 | If work has to block it, a second way up and down has to be set up and used. | 1926.1051(a)(3) | exact: "When work must be performed or equipment must be used such that free passage at that point of access is restricted," "a second point of access shall be provided and used." |

| 2.2 | Every open side also needs a stair rail. | 1926.1052(c)(1) | paraphrase: and one stairrail system along each unprotected side or edge. OSHA 3124 exact: "must be installed along each unprotected side or edge." |

| 2.5 | A handrail or top rail has to hold at least 200 pounds pushed down or out near the top. | 1926.1052(c)(5) | paraphrase: handrails and stairrail top rails withstand, without failure, at least 200 pounds applied within 2 inches of the top edge, in any downward or outward direction |
| 3.1 | Metal pan treads and landings get secured in place before they're filled with concrete or other material. | 1926.1052(a)(5) | paraphrase: metal pan landings and treads secured in place before filling with concrete or other material. | 3.2 | Unless you're building the stairs, stay off pan stairs that haven't been filled yet, unless they're temporarily filled or covered with solid material up to the top of the pan. | 1926.1052(b)(1) | paraphrase: except during stairway construction, foot traffic is prohibited on pan stairs awaiting later fill unless temporarily filled/covered with solid material at least to the top edge of the pan |
| 3.3 | Same for skeleton metal stairs. | 3.4 | Temporary treads are wood or other solid material, and they cover the full width and depth of the step. | 1926.1052(b)(3) | OSHA 3124 exact: "Temporary treads must be made of wood or other solid material and installed the full width and depth of the stair." |
| 4.1 | Slippery stairs get fixed before anyone uses them to get to another level. | 1926.1052(a)(7) | paraphrase: slippery conditions on stairways eliminated before the stairways are used to reach other levels |
| 4.2 | No sticking-out nails or other dangerous projections on any part of the stairs. | 1926.1052(a)(6) | paraphrase: all parts of a stairway free of hazardous projections, such as protruding nails.
