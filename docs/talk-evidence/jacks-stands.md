# jacks-stands evidence

Std = OSHA 1910.244(a), Jacks (https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.244). General
industry; covers jacks of all types. It does not mention jack stands.
NE = Nebraska FACE 99NE018, in cooperation with NIOSH, hosted by NIOSH (https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/face/stateface/ne/99ne018.html).
IA = Iowa FACE 95IA033, hosted by NIOSH (https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/face/stateface/ia/95ia033.html).

| English sentence | Source | Supporting quote |
|---|---|---|
| A jack is for lifting, not for holding. | NE / Std (a)(2)(iii) | paraphrase: bottle jacks were used to lift vehicles but "were not supposed to be used to suspend the load"; Std: raised load must be cribbed/blocked/secured at once |
| In one fatality report, a bus slid off a bottle jack and killed the mechanic under it. | NE | paraphrase: a tour bus "slipped off a 12-ton bottle jack" and landed on the mechanic, who died |
| Jack stands were right there in the shop. | NE | paraphrase: "supported with adequate jack stands" or wood blocks would likely have prevented the death; both were available on site |
| Before you use a jack, make sure its rating is enough to lift and hold the load. | Std (a)(1)(i) | exact: "The operator shall make sure that the jack used has a rating sufficient to lift and sustain the load." |
| Every jack has to have its rated load marked on it, permanently and where you can see it. | Std (a)(1)(ii) | exact: "The rated load shall be legibly and permanently marked in a prominent location on the jack by casting, stamping, or other suitable means" |
| Lift and secure the vehicle the way the maker's instructions say. | NE | exact: "Employers should ensure equipment is lifted and secured in accordance with manufacturers' instructions." |
| If the instructions aren't around, ask for them. | NE | paraphrase: manufacturer information "should be obtained and made available to all personnel using the jacks" |
| If the ground isn't firm, block under the base of the jack. | Std (a)(2)(i) | exact: "In the absence of a firm foundation, the base of the jack shall be blocked." |
| If the cap could slip, put a block between the cap and the load. | Std (a)(2)(i) | exact: "If there is a possibility of slippage of the cap, a block shall be placed in between the cap and the load." |
| Watch the stop indicator and don't go past its limit. | Std (a)(2)(ii) | exact: "The operator shall watch the stop indicator, which shall be kept clean, in order to determine the limit of travel. The indicated limit shall not be overrun." |
| Once the load is up, crib it, block it, or secure it right away. | Std (a)(2)(iii) | exact: "After the load has been raised, it shall be cribbed, blocked, or otherwise secured at once." |
| Before anyone gets under it, it has to be blocked or set on adequate jack stands. | NE | exact: "Employers should ensure all equipment, with a potential to fall, is properly blocked/chocked up prior to getting under it." (FACE recommendation; "has to" read as the safe practice, Std (a)(2)(iii) supplies the legal duty to secure the load) |
| In the bus case, both stands and blocks were on site and weren't used. | NE | paraphrase: jack stands and wood blocks were both available on site |
| A forklift is not a stand. Don't work under a car held up only by a forklift. | IA | exact: "Auto mechanics should not work under cars when they are elevated and supported only by a forklift." / "Forklifts do not provide adequate safety when working under a raised car." |
| Jacks get a thorough inspection at least every 6 months when used in one place. | Std (a)(2)(vi)(a) | paraphrase: thoroughly inspected at intervals per service conditions, no less often than "for constant or intermittent use at one locality, once every 6 months" |
| They also get one before and after any abnormal load or shock, and when sent out for special work and when they come back. | Std (a)(2)(vi)(b)-(c) | paraphrase: "For jacks sent out of shop for special work, when sent out and when returned" / "For a jack subjected to abnormal load or shock, immediately before and immediately thereafter." |
| A jack that's out of order gets tagged and isn't used until it's repaired. | Std (a)(2)(viii) | exact: "Jacks which are out of order shall be tagged accordingly, and shall not be used until repairs are made." |
| A hydraulic jack that sees freezing weather needs the right antifreeze fluid. | Std (a)(2)(iv) | exact: "Hydraulic jacks exposed to freezing temperatures shall be supplied with an adequate antifreeze liquid." |
