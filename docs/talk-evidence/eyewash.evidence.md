Dropped for lack of a source or to avoid overstating:
- ANSI Z358.1 timings (15-minute flush, 10 seconds, 100 feet): the only OSHA page giving them is the July 20, 1992 interpretation (osha.gov/laws-regs/standardinterpretations/1992-07-20-5), which is marked "OSHA Archive Document... may no longer represent OSHA Policy." The Nov 1, 2002 interpretation (standardinterpretations/2002-11-01) says OSHA "has often referred employers to ANSI Z358.1 as a recognized source of guidance" and that "ANSI standards become mandatory OSHA standards only if, and when, OSHA adopts them," but gives no timings. So no timings are in the talk.
- "Hold your eyes open for 15 minutes" / "remove contact lenses": no current OSHA/NIOSH page fetched states them. NIOSH NPG says only "large amounts of water" and "occasionally lifting the lower and upper lids."
- "Tell your supervisor which chemical it was" and "check the eyewash weekly": no source fetched.
- OSHA 3317 (first-aid program best practices) was fetched; it does not cover eyewash use.
