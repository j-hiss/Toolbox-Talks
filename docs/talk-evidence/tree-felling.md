# tree-felling — evidence

Pages fetched 2026-10-07 with WebFetch (summaries plus short exact quotes; curl to osha.gov is blocked).
HB3731 = https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHAHB3731.pdf · 3752 = https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHA3752.pdf · 3940 = https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHA3940.pdf · HS = https://www.osha.gov/tree-care/hazards-solutions

**Scope note.** OSHA has no tree-care-specific standard (https://www.osha.gov/tree-care/rulemaking: a "Tree Care Operations" standard for Part 1910 is still at the proposed-rule stage). https://www.osha.gov/tree-care/standards says "The tree care industry must comply with all the general industry standards (29 CFR 1910)" and does not mention 1910.266. 1910.266 covers "all types of logging" ((b)(1)); "logging operations" = "Operations associated with felling and moving trees and logs from the stump to the point of delivery..." ((c)). Whether it reaches a given tree-care removal is not settled by any page fetched, so the talk labels the 1910.266 lines as logging rules and gives the tree-care distances from OSHA guidance (HB3731).
