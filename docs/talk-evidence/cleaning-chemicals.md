# cleaning-chemicals evidence

Std = OSHA 1910.1200 (https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1200).
3512 = OSHA/NIOSH InfoSheet "Protecting Workers Who Use Cleaning Chemicals" (https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHA3512.pdf).
3569 = OSHA "Protect Yourself: Cleaning Chemicals and Your Health" (https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHA_3569.pdf).

Scope note: 1910.1200(b)(6)(ix) exempts consumer products used in the workplace the way a consumer would use them
(same duration and frequency). Talk therefore says "hazardous" chemicals/products and does not claim every cleaner is
covered. Portable-container exception 1910.1200(f)(8) exists, so the talk does not say every spray bottle must be
labeled.

OSHA "green cleaning" pages: https://www.osha.gov/green-jobs has no cleaning content. The green-cleaning line comes from
3512 instead.
