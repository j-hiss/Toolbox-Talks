# lead evidence

Paragraph notes: (h) housekeeping and (i) hygiene are as given in the brief. Most (i) hygiene duties apply where airborne exposure is **above the PEL** (50 µg/m3, 8-hr TWA, (c)(1)); handwashing facilities (i)(5)(i) apply to employees exposed to lead generally. Lines that rest on above-PEL paragraphs are either stated conditionally in the talk or also backed by OSHA 3142 general guidance. The tasks presumed high are (d)(2)(i)(A) (up to 10x PEL) and (d)(2)(iv) (over 2,500 µg/m3 = 50x PEL); (d)(2)(iv) is not the "heat gun/scraping" tier.
