Also read: OSHA interpretation 1980-09-12 (https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/standardinterpretations/1980-09-12-0), confirming 1926.604 requires overhead and rear canopy guards on rider-operated site-clearing equipment; not cited in the talk.

**Scope note.** 1926.604 is the construction rule (Part 1926). Its full text is (a)(1), (a)(2), (a)(2)(i), (a)(2)(ii); (b) is "[Reserved]". 1926.25 is also construction and its (a) is limited to work areas, passageways and stairs "in and around buildings or other structures". There is no OSHA general-industry standard for site clearing or brush removal by landscapers; for non-construction work the talk uses OSHA's landscaping guidance page (LH), and labels it as advice. Rollover/seat-belt/parking/backing rules are already in `equipment-rops` and `struck-vehicle`; chipper, chainsaw and tree-felling have their own talks; buried utilities has `underground-utilities`. This talk does not repeat them.
