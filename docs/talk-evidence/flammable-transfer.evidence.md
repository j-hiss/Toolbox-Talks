Overlap note: `fueling` already covers vehicle fueling at service stations, gas cans and nozzle contact; `oil-fires` covers well-site ignition sources and "bond and ground hoses". This talk is narrowed to container/drum transfer and tank-truck loading (1910.106(e) and (f)(3)).

Scope note: 1910.106(e) applies to industrial plants where flammable liquid use is incidental to the principal business, or used only in unit physical operations; "This paragraph shall not apply to chemical plants, refineries or distilleries." (e)(1)(i). (f) is bulk plants. The talk says "in plants and shops covered by this rule" and "at a bulk plant loading rack" rather than claiming these bind every well site or truck stop. The grounding, point-of-use, and dome-bonding rules apply to Category 1 or 2 liquids, or Category 3 below 100 °F; the talk says "gasoline and other low-flashpoint liquids" / "low-flashpoint liquids". Calling gasoline low-flashpoint is common knowledge, not from a fetched page; reviewer may prefer "the more flammable liquids".
