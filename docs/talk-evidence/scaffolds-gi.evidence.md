# scaffolds-gi evidence

General industry has no scaffold rule of its own beyond a pointer: 1910.27(a) says "Scaffolds used in general industry must meet the requirements in 29 CFR part 1926, subpart L." So every working rule in this talk is a construction rule (1926.451) that applies in general industry through 1910.27(a). Source labels say so. Both pages fetched from osha.gov standardnumber pages on 2026-10-08. OSHA Scaffolding Standards page lists 1910.27 as the general industry scaffold standard.

Overlap check: existing `scaffold` (con, 1926.451/1926.454) covers construction crews. This talk is for facilities and manufacturing, leads with the 1910.27(a) pointer, and keeps to a short subset (competent person, footing, planks, loads, access, 10-ft fall protection). Training (1926.454) is not covered because it was not fetched.

Notes and corrections:
- Assignment pointed to 1910.27(a) plus 1926.451 "as referenced": confirmed. 1910.27(a) is a one-sentence cross-reference to all of Subpart L (1926.450-1926.454), not only 1926.451.
- Cross-brace rule is the last sentence of 1926.451(e)(1). Unstable objects is (c)(2)(ii) (not (c)(2)(iv), which is front-end loaders). Boxes/barrels is (f)(14).
- Dropped: guardrail top-rail heights (1926.451(g)(4)(ii) splits 38-45 in. and 36-45 in. by date placed in service; too conditional for a short talk). Dropped training (1926.454), not fetched.
