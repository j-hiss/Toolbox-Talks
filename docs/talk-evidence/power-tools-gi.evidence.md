**Overlap note.** Library has `hand-tools-con` (construction, 1926.300-.304), `grinders` (mfg/auto, 1910.215 + 3080), `compressed-air` and `mowers`. This talk is the general-industry version, built on 1910.242(a) and 1910.243; grinder and mower content is kept to one or two lines each.

**OSHA 3080 not used.** The assignment named OSHA 3080 (Hand and Power Tools). It would not load (provenance block on https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/osha3080.pdf and https://www.osha.gov/Publications/osha3080.pdf), so its practice lines (never carry by the cord, disconnect before changing blades, keep bystanders back, tag damaged tools "Do Not Use", wet locations) are NOT in this talk. A checker who can load 3080 could add them; 3080 quotes already verified for other talks are in hand-tools-con.evidence.md.
