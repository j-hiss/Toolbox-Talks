Corrections to the paragraph numbers given:
- 1910.22(b) is "Loads" (each surface supports its maximum intended load), not aisles. The current 1910.22 does not mention aisles; the aisle rules are in 1910.176(a). Used 1910.22(a)(2)-(3) (floors clean, dry, free of spills) instead.
- 1910.37 does not itself require exit doors to be unlocked; that rule is 1910.36(d)(1).

Dropped for lack of a source / scope:
- Exit sign letter size (6 inches, 3/4-inch strokes, 1910.37(b)(7)) and 5 foot-candles: accurate but not useful for a crew; left out of the spoken text.
- Exit door swing direction (1910.36(e)(2)) and headroom (1910.36(g)(1)): accurate, left out for length.
