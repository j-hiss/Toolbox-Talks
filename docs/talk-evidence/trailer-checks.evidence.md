# trailer-checks evidence

Overlap note: the existing `loading-docks` talk already covers chocks, fixed jacks, the floor check, dock plates and driving inside the trailer. This talk is narrowed to what's new: the trailer walk-around from OSHA TIB 00-07-31 (rear impact guard, landing gear, undercarriage, sides, rivets, walls, floor signs) and what to do when damage is found. The hold-it-still section is kept to two short lines as the lead-in.

Sources fetched by this writer (WebFetch):
- TIB = OSHA TIB 00-07-31 (July 31, 2000), https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/tib20000731.pdf. TIB is advisory: its Important Information box says it creates no new legal obligations and that failing to follow it is not, in itself, a General Duty Clause violation. The talk marks rule lines as "OSHA rule" only where they come from 1910.178.
- 1910.178 (k)(1), (k)(3), (m)(7): the osha.gov standardnumber page could not be fetched in this session (tool provenance block). Text verified on eCFR, https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/subtitle-B/chapter-XVII/part-1910/subpart-N/section-1910.178. Fact-checker: please confirm on osha.gov.
- STD = OSHA Trucking Industry: Standards, https://osha.gov/trucking-industry/standards.
- LU = OSHA Trucking Industry: Loading and Unloading, https://www.osha.gov/trucking-industry/loading-unloading.

Paragraph-number corrections to the assignment:
- Assignment gave (k)(1) and (m)(7). Both confirmed. Added (k)(3) (fixed jacks for uncoupled semitrailers), which is the paragraph that covers landing-gear support; (m)(7) repeats it.
- Scope: OSHA's Trucking Standards page says DOT's brake rule covers trailer wheel chocking on interstate trucks (OSHA does not enforce there under 4(b)(1)); OSHA keeps authority over intrastate trucks. The loading-docks evidence file also records an OSHA statement that FMCSA rules preempt (k)(1) and (m)(7) for CMVs; I did not re-fetch that page. The talk says it plainly in one line.

- TIB check points 6, 7, 9 (rear doors, door hinges, roof): fetched, left out for length.
- "Dock locks" / vehicle restraints as a required method: only mentioned in TIB as something ICC-bar damage can defeat; no rule claimed.
