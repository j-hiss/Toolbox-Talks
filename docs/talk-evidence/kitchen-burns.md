# kitchen-burns evidence

Guidance page: OSHA Young Worker Safety in Restaurants eTool, Cooking, https://www.osha.gov/etools/young-workers-restaurant-safety/cooking ("eTool Cooking"). The page is written for young workers; the talk uses only the general safe practices and never mentions age.

| English sentence | Source | Supporting quote |
|---|---|---|
| Stoves, grills, steamers, and fryers can all burn you. So can the hot oil, grease, steam, and hot food that come off them. | guidance: eTool Cooking | paraphrase: hazard "Hot oil, grease, and steam from hot surfaces, hot food and beverages, and equipment such as stoves, grills, steamers, and fryers." |
| Never let water or ice get into hot oil. | guidance: eTool Cooking | exact: "Do not spill water or ice into oil." |
| Don't pour the extra ice from a fry bag into the fryer. | guidance | exact: "Do not pour excess ice from fry packages into the fryer." |
| Don't overfill the fryer or dump in too many frozen fries at once. That makes hot oil splash and bubble over. | guidance | exact: "Do not overfill or pour excessive amounts of frozen fries into deep fryer at one time." / "Overfilling causes excessive splashing and bubbling over of hot oil." |
| Don't move or strain hot oil. Wait until it's cool. | guidance | exact: "Do not move or strain hot oil containers; wait until the oil is cool!" |
| Keep your drinks away from the fryer. One bump can knock a drink into the oil and cause a flare-up. | guidance | exact: "Do not store employee drinks by deep fryers. They could be easily bumped into the hot oil and cause a flare-up." |
| Open ovens and steamers from the side, with the door between you and the steam. | guidance | exact: "Open ovens or steamers by standing to the side, keeping the door between you and the open steamer." |
| When steamers are stacked, open the top one first. | guidance | exact: "Open the top steamer first when steamers are stacked..." |
| Lift lids away from your face. | guidance | exact: "Uncover a container of steaming materials by lifting the lid open away from your face." |
| Don't use a wet cloth to lift a lid off a hot pot. | guidance | exact: "Do not use a wet cloth to lift lids from hot pots." |
| Treat every pot, pot handle, and utensil in a pot as hot, and use mitts. | guidance | exact: "Assume that pots, pot handles, and utensils in pots are hot and use oven mitts when handling them." |
| Don't let handles stick out past the counter or stove front. | guidance | exact: "Do not allow pot handles or cooking utensils to stick out from counters or stove fronts." |
| Get help to move a heavy pot of hot liquid off the burner. | guidance | exact: "Ask for help when moving or carrying a heavy pot of hot liquid off the burner." |
| Keep the grill free of grease buildup that could catch fire. | guidance | exact: "Keep grilling surfaces clean and free from grease accumulations that might ignite and cause a fire." |
| Don't clean the vents over a grill while it's hot. | guidance | exact: "Do not clean vents over grill areas if the grill is hot." |
| Never throw water on a grease fire. It makes the fire worse. | guidance | exact: "Never throw water on a grease fire; this will make the fire worse." |
| A hot oil or grease fire takes a Class K extinguisher. | guidance | exact: "Extinguish hot oil/grease fires by using a class K fire extinguisher." |
| If your clothes catch fire: stop, drop, and roll. | guidance | exact: "Know that if you catch fire, STOP, DROP and ROLL." |
| Wear long sleeves and long pants when you cook, and a clean, dry apron. | guidance | exact: "Do wear long-sleeved cotton shirts and pants when cooking." / "A clean, dry, properly worn apron or uniform can protect you from burns and hot oil splashes." |
| Wear slip-resistant shoes, not canvas or open-toed. | guidance | exact: "Wear sturdy footwear that is slip resistant and not canvas or open-toed to protect the feet in case hot liquids are spilled on shoes." |
| Clean up spills right away. | guidance + OSHA 1910.22(a)(2) | exact: "Clean up spills immediately to avoid falls." 1910.22(a)(2): "The floor of each workroom is maintained in a clean and, to the extent feasible, in a dry condition." |
| A slip near the line can put you into hot oil or onto a hot surface. | guidance | exact: "Keep floor surfaces clean and dry to prevent slipping or falling onto hot surfaces." / "Do not store oil on floors by grill area. Someone could slip and fall into the oil." |
| Where your hands are exposed to burns, your company has to choose the right hand protection and require you to use it. | OSHA 1910.138(a) | exact: "Employers shall select and require employees to use appropriate hand protection when employees' hands are exposed to hazards such as ... thermal burns..." |
| Use the mitts and gloves you're given. | guidance | exact: "Use oven mitts or pot holders when handling hot items" / "wear all protective equipment provided for your use while preparing hot items." |

Left out: "deep fat fryers are the leading cause of burns" (only seen in a model summary, not confirmed as an exact quote); heat illness 105-110°F (off topic); carbon monoxide from portable fryers (off topic); "higher quality oils" (product choice).
