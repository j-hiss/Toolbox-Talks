# lightning evidence

FS = OSHA/NOAA fact sheet FS-3863 "Lightning Safety When Working Outdoors", https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/publications/OSHA3863.pdf (fetched 2026-10-07; the copy served is dated 06/2021). The weather.gov 05/2016 copy was not needed.

| English sentence | Source | Supporting quote |
|---|---|---|
| When thunder roars, go indoors! | guidance: FS | exact: "When thunder roars, go indoors!" |
| If you hear thunder, even a distant rumble, get to a safe place right away. | guidance: FS | exact: "If you hear thunder, even a distant rumble, get to a safe place immediately." |
| Lightning can strike up to 10 miles from any rain. | guidance: FS | exact: "Lightning is unpredictable and can strike outside the heaviest rainfall areas or even up to 10 miles from any rainfall." |
| Before outdoor work starts, your supervisor should check the weather report. | guidance: FS | exact: "Prior to beginning any outdoor work, employers and supervisors should check NOAA weather reports" |
| Roofing, scaffold work and heavy equipment are on OSHA's higher-risk list. | guidance: FS | exact: list of jobs "higher risk for lightning hazards" includes "Roofing", "Construction (e.g., scaffolding)", "Heavy equipment operation" |
| If a storm looks like it's coming, don't start any task you can't stop quickly. | guidance: FS | exact: "If signs of approaching thunderstorms occur, workers should not begin any task they cannot quickly stop." |
| Warning systems help, but none of them can catch the first strike or predict every strike. | guidance: FS | exact: "no systems can detect the 'first strike,' detect all lightning, or predict lightning strikes." (paraphrase: "predict lightning strikes" rendered as "predict every strike") |
| The safest place is a fully enclosed building with electrical wiring and plumbing. | guidance: FS | exact: "NOAA recommends seeking out fully enclosed buildings with electrical wiring and plumbing." |
| No building close by? Get in a hard-topped metal vehicle and roll the windows up. | guidance: FS | exact: "hard-topped metal vehicles with rolled up windows" (when safe buildings are not accessible) |
| Inside, stay off electrical equipment and cords, plumbing fixtures, and corded phones, except in an emergency. | guidance: FS | exact: "as long as you are not in contact with anything that can conduct electricity" "(e.g., electrical equipment or cords, plumbing fixtures, corded phones)"; "After hearing thunder, do not use corded phones, except in an emergency." |
| Sheds, tents, pavilions and covered porches are not safe shelter. | guidance: FS | exact: "Do not shelter in sheds, pavilions, tents, or covered porches" |
| Get off the roof, the ladder, the scaffold and big equipment. | guidance: FS | exact: "Avoid isolated tall trees, hilltops, utility poles, cell phone towers, cranes, large equipment," "ladders, scaffolding, or rooftops." |
| Lightning tends to hit the tallest thing around. Don't be it. | guidance: FS | exact: "Lightning is likely to strike the tallest objects in a given area—you should not be the tallest object." |
| Stay away from metal, wiring, fencing and water. | guidance: FS | exact: "Stay away from all metal objects, equipment, and surfaces that can conduct electricity."; "Avoid wiring, plumbing, and fencing."; "Avoid water, and immediately get out of and away from bodies of water" |
| Stay out of open areas, and never lie flat on the ground. | guidance: FS | exact: "Avoid open areas, such as fields. Never lie flat on the ground." |
| Scaffold work stops during storms or high winds, unless a competent person decides it's safe and the crew is protected by fall arrest or secured wind screens. | OSHA 1926.451(f)(12) | exact: "Work on or from scaffolds is prohibited during storms or high winds"; paraphrase: unless a competent person determines it is safe and employees are protected by a personal fall arrest system or wind screens; "Wind screens shall not be used unless the scaffold is secured against the anticipated wind forces imposed." |
| Stay in the shelter or vehicle for at least 30 minutes after the last sound of thunder. | guidance: FS | exact: "Remain in the shelter for at least 30 minutes after hearing the last sound of thunder."; "Remain in the vehicle for at least 30 minutes after hearing the last sound of thunder." |
| Hear thunder again? That wasn't the last one.
