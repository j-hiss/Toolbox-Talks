# Evidence: animal-illness

No OSHA standard: OSHA Avian Influenza Standards page: "There is no specific OSHA standard covering avian influenza viruses." The General Duty Clause "may be cited where uncontrolled occupational hazards are present and no other OSHA standard is applicable." OSHA Hantavirus page: "There are no specific OSHA standards for hantavirus." Everything below is OSHA/NIOSH guidance (kind "guidance"). The avian flu standards page lists 1910.132/1910.134 as "may be applicable" and says nothing about how they apply to farms, so the talk does not state PPE as a legal rule and words the company's role as "should".

Overlap: "field-sanitation" covers handwashing facilities; "livestock" covers handling injuries. This talk is about infection only.

CDC: no CDC page is cited (CDC was not named in the assignment). OSHA's alert refers to CDC protocols; the talk doesn't.
