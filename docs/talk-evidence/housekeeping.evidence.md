# housekeeping evidence

Paragraph notes: 1926.25 has only (a)-(c), as given. 1926.151 has **no** oily-rag or combustible-scrap text; its relevant part is (c) open yard storage, and only (c)(5) was used. The oily-rag container rule is 1926.252(e). Cord rules are not in 1926.25; they come from 1926.416(b)(2) and (e). The nail-pulling line comes from 1926.250(b)(8)(i) and a 2006 OSHA interpretation letter.
