# asbestos evidence

Paragraph notes: the PACM, Class II, surfacing-material and TSI definitions are in (b). The "presumed" rules are in (k)(1)(i). The roofing work practices are (g)(8)(ii)(A)-(H). (k) is "Communication of hazards"; the duty to report newly found material is (k)(4), and the identify-before-work duty is (k)(3)(i). Class II competent-person supervision is (g)(7)(i). The roofing exclusion for asphalt coatings/cements/mastics is (a)(8). Class II respirator rule is (h)(1)(iii) (not used in the talk).

Dropped or kept out:
- The (g)(8)(ii)(H) exception (intact roofing under 25 sq ft, manual methods, no visible dust: no wet methods/HEPA needed). Accurate but too conditional to read aloud; left out rather than half-stated.
- (g)(8)(ii)(D) power roof cutter on built-up roofs with asbestos felts: HEPA dust collection. Left out for length.
- Respirator rule (h)(1)(iii) for Class II work not done wet: has a sloped-roof exception; left out rather than overstate.
- "No dry sanding" of roofing: the standard's sanding ban is for flooring ((g)(8)(i)(A)); no roofing sanding ban was found, so the talk states the flooring ban only.
- A general worker "stop work" duty: not in the standard (see flagged line).
- Asbestos in roofing felts/built-up roofs as a presumption: not presumed; only mentioned in (g)(8)(ii)(D) as a case.
