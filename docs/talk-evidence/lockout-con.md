Scope notes for the reviewer:
- Construction has no general lockout/tagout standard like 1910.147. The construction rules used are 1926.417 (tagging circuits), 1926.416(a)(1), 1926.702(j) (concrete and masonry equipment), and 1926.600(a)(3) (blocking/lowering equipment). 1926.417 itself requires **tags** and rendering inoperative; it does not mention locks. The lock steps ("each worker puts on their own lock", testing, stored energy, re-energizing) come from the OSHA Focus Four Electrocution Instructor Guide (guidance), and are phrased as steps, not as citations of a rule.
- Assignment paragraph numbers confirmed: 1926.417 has only (a)-(c); 1926.702(j) has (j)(1)-(2); 1926.600(a)(3) is (a)(3)(i)-(ii).
