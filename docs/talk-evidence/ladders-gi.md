# ladders-gi evidence

General industry ladder rules are 29 CFR 1910.23 (Subpart D). Scope (a): "This section covers all ladders, except when the ladder is: Used in emergency operations ... or Designed into or is an integral part of machines or equipment." Most of 1910.23 is written as "The employer must ensure ..."; the talk gives the crew the action and says "Your company has to" where the duty is the employer's (inspection).

Corrections to the paragraph numbers given in the assignment (checked against the osha.gov text):
- No top step / cap of stepladder is **(c)(8)**, not (c)(4). (c)(4) is stable and level surfaces unless secured.
- "Secured" is **(c)(4)**, **(c)(7)** (traffic areas) and **(c)(9)** (slippery surfaces). (c)(5) is "No portable single rail ladders are used."
- (c)(3) is the maximum intended load rule, not ladder "uses". Intended use is **(b)(8)**.
- One hand on the ladder is **(b)(12)**, not (b)(13). (b)(13) is not carrying objects that could cause a loss of balance.
- (b)(11) face the ladder: correct.
- 1910.25 is stairways and does not cover step stools; step stools are defined in 1910.21(b) and covered by 1910.23. Stepstool step spacing is 1910.23(b)(3) (8-12 inches), not used in the talk.

Talk uses the standard only. OSHA 3662 stepladder fact sheet loaded but is construction-focused; not cited.
