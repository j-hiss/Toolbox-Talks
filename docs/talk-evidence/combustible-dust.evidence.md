# combustible-dust evidence

Scope note: OSHA's Combustible Dust standards page lists 15 standards that "address certain aspects" of combustible dust hazards and says the General Duty Clause "may apply" where no standard does. The NEP (CPL 03-00-008) says combustible dust hazards are "covered by several OSHA standards and, where those standards do not apply, the general duty clause." There is no single combustible dust standard. NEP also notes 1910.22(a) does not apply to agricultural operations (not relevant to this mfg talk).
