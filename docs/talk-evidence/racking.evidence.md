# racking evidence

Std = OSHA 1910.176 (https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.176).
WH = OSHA Warehousing, Hazards and Solutions (https://www.osha.gov/warehousing/hazards-solutions).
3220 = OSHA 3220-10N (2004), Worker Safety Series: Warehousing (https://www.osha.gov/Publications/3220_Warehouse.pdf).

| English sentence | Source | Supporting quote |
|---|---|---|
| Racks hold a lot of weight, often over people's heads. | WH | paraphrase: "Chronic neck pain from prolonged looking up to store units"; "Provide and ensure workers use head protection if they are exposed to falling object hazards." |
| When storage goes wrong, loads slide, fall, or the whole rack collapses. | 1910.176(b); WH | exact: "stable and secure against sliding or collapse." / "Inspect and maintain shelving and racking to prevent collapse." / "to prevent falling hazards." |
| The rule is simple: storage can't create a hazard. | 1910.176(b) | exact: "Storage of material shall not create a hazard." |
| Bags, boxes and bundles stored in tiers have to be stacked, blocked, interlocked and limited in height, so they won't slide or collapse. | 1910.176(b) | exact: "Bags, containers, bundles, etc., stored in tiers shall be stacked, blocked, interlocked and limited in height" / "so that they are stable and secure against sliding or collapse." |
| Stack loads evenly and straight. | 3220 | exact: "Stack loads evenly and straight;" |
| Put heavier loads on the lower or middle shelves. | 3220; WH | exact: "Place heavier loads on lower or middle shelves." |
| Stack loose, unboxed material so it can't fall. | WH | exact: "Properly stack loose/unboxed materials to prevent falling hazards." |
| Every shelf and rack has a load capacity. Don't go over it. | WH | exact: "Ensure that storage shelving and rack load capacities are not exceeded." |
| Racks are inspected and maintained so they don't collapse. | WH | exact: "Inspect and maintain shelving and racking to prevent collapse." |
| If you see damage, like a rack that got hit by a forklift, report it right away. | WH | paraphrase: "If damage occurs, immediately isolate the affected area." / "Install rack upright guards to prevent damage from incidental forklift contact." |
| When a rack is damaged, that area gets blocked off right away. Stay out of it. | WH | exact: "If damage occurs, immediately isolate the affected area." |
| Take items off a shelf one at a time. | 3220 | exact: "Remove one object at a time from shelves;" |
| Where forklifts and other equipment run, aisles need safe clearance, including at docks, doorways and turns. | 1910.176(a) | exact: "Where mechanical handling equipment is used, sufficient safe clearances shall be allowed for aisles, at loading docks," / "through doorways and wherever turns or passage must be made." |
| Keep aisles clear and in good repair. Nothing left in or across an aisle that could cause a hazard. | 1910.176(a) | exact: "Aisles and passageways shall be kept clear and in good repair," / "with no obstruction across or in aisles that could create a hazard." |
| Permanent aisles are marked. | 1910.176(a) | exact: "Permanent aisles and passageways shall be appropriately marked." |
| Don't leave pallets or product in them. | 1910.176(a) | paraphrase: "no obstruction across or in aisles that could create a hazard." |
| Keep storage areas free of piled-up material that could trip someone, start a fire, or attract pests. | 1910.176(c) | exact: "Storage areas shall be kept free from accumulation of materials that constitute hazards from tripping," / "fire, explosion, or pest harborage." |
| Need something up high? Use a ladder. Never set a ladder on a box, barrel or pallet to get more height. | WH (Ladders) | exact: "Never place a portable ladder on a box, barrel, pallet, or other unstable base to obtain additional height." |
| Keep three points of contact on a ladder. | WH (Ladders) | exact: "Maintain "3 points of contact" when climbing up or down ladders" |
| Never step on the top cap of a stepladder. | WH (Ladders) | exact: "Never use top caps of step ladders as a step." |
| If things could fall on you, wear the head protection you're given. | WH (General Considerations) | exact: "Provide and ensure workers use head protection if they are exposed to falling object hazards." |
