## Hook
- "A dropped wrench doesn't care who's standing under it. Working up top means protecting the people below."
  - guidance: eTool, "You are at risk from falling objects when you are beneath cranes, scaffolds, etc."; "Secure tools and materials to prevent them from falling on people below."

## Hard hats, plus one more layer
- "If something could fall or fly and hit you in the head, you wear a hard hat."
  - 1926.100(a): "Employees working in areas where there is a possible danger of head injury from impact, or from falling or flying objects, or from electrical shock and burns, shall be protected by protective helmets."; 1926.501(c): "the employer shall have each employee wear a hard hat"
- "A hard hat isn't enough by itself. When people are exposed to falling objects, the job also needs one of these: toe boards, screens, or guardrails; a canopy; or a barricaded area nobody enters."
  - 1926.501(c): "When an employee is exposed to falling objects, the employer shall have each employee wear a hard hat and shall implement one of the following measures:"; (c)(1): "Erect toeboards, screens, or guardrail systems..."; (c)(2): "Erect a canopy structure..."; (c)(3): "Barricade the area to which objects could fall, prohibit employees from entering the barricaded area,"

## Toe boards and screens
- "A toe board is at least 3 and a half inches tall, sits no more than a quarter inch above the deck, and has no openings bigger than 1 inch."
  - 1926.502(j)(3): "Toeboards shall be a minimum of 3½ inches (9 cm) in vertical height ... They shall have not more than ¼ inch (0.6 cm) clearance above the walking/working surface. They shall be solid or have openings not over 1 inch (2.5 cm) in greatest dimension."
- "It has to hold at least 50 pounds of force without failing"
  - 1926.502(j)(2): "Toeboards shall be capable of withstanding, without failure, a force of at least 50 pounds (222 N)"
- "and run far enough along the edge to protect the people below."
  - 1926.502(j)(1): "shall be erected along the edge of the overhead walking/working surface for a distance sufficient to protect employees below."
- "If tools or materials are piled higher than the toe board, put up paneling or screening up to the top rail or midrail."
  - 1926.502(j)(4): "Where tools, equipment, or materials are piled higher than the top edge of a toeboard, paneling or screening shall be erected from the walking/working surface or toeboard to the top of a guardrail system's top rail or midrail"

## Canopies and barricades
- "A canopy has to be strong enough not to collapse, and strong enough that falling objects can't punch through it."
  - 1926.502(j)(8): "Canopies, when used as falling object protection, shall be strong enough to prevent collapse and to prevent penetration by any objects which may fall onto the canopy."
- "With a canopy or a barricade, keep materials far enough back from the edge that they won't go over if they get bumped."
  - 1926.501(c)(2): "keep potential fall objects far enough from the edge of the higher level so that those objects would not go over the edge if they were accidentally displaced"; (c)(3): "keep objects that may fall far enough away from the edge of a higher level so that those objects would not go over the edge if they were accidentally displaced."
- "A barricaded area means nobody goes in."
  - 1926.501(c)(3): "prohibit employees from entering the barricaded area"
- "Respect the barricade and the warning signs."
  - guidance: eTool, "Barricade hazard areas and post warning signs."

## Keep it secured up top
- "On a roof, don't store materials or equipment within 6 feet of the edge unless there are guardrails."
  - 1926.502(j)(7): "During the performance of roofing work: Materials and equipment shall not be stored within 6 feet (1.8 m) of a roof edge unless guardrails are erected at the edge."
- "Anything stacked near the edge has to be stable and able to stand on its own."
  - 1926.502(j)(7): "Materials which are piled, grouped, or stacked near a roof edge shall be stable and self-supporting."
- "Secure your tools and materials so they can't fall on people below."
  - guidance: eTool, "Secure tools and materials to prevent them from falling on people below."
- "Stack materials so they can't slide, fall, or collapse."
  - guidance: eTool, "Stack materials to prevent sliding, falling, or collapse."
- "On the ground, don't work under loads being moved."
  - guidance: eTool, "Avoid working underneath loads being moved."
