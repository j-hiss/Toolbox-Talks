Standard note for reviewer: OSHA has no general-industry standard written specifically for wood chippers outside logging (1910.266 mentions chippers in logging, e.g. face protection). The talk relies on SHIB 04-16-2008 and OSHA 3940. The one standard cited is 1910.147 (lockout/tagout), which the SHIB names for servicing: "Before beginning any servicing operation, lockout/tagout procedures must be utilized to control hazardous energy". 1910.147(a)(2)(ii) covers servicing during normal production only when a guard is removed or bypassed, or a body part goes into the point of operation; clearing a jammed chute fits the second case. `code` is set to "1910.147".

## Hook
- "A chipper's feed rollers grab whatever they touch."
  - guidance: SHIB: the pull-in hazard is described throughout (glove cuff snagged on a branch, operator pulled in; tray extensions make it "more difficult for employees to reach into the feed rollers"). Reviewer note: "grab whatever they touch" is plain-language framing, not a quote. Softer option: "A chipper's feed rollers can pull you in fast."
- "In one OSHA case, an operator standing right in front of the infeed chute was pulled in, maybe after his glove cuff caught on a branch."
  - guidance: SHIB (Denver, Colorado incident): "the operator was standing directly in front of the infeed chute." The bulletin says a glove cuff may have snagged a branch and pulled him in (paraphrase; "maybe" keeps the bulletin's hedge).

## Before you start
- "Inspect and test the chipper at the start of every shift."
  - guidance: SHIB: "inspect and test the chipper at the start of each workshift" "to ensure that all parts and safety devices are functioning properly" (exact excerpt)
- "Make sure the guards, controls, and emergency shut-offs work."
  - guidance: OSHA 3940: "Make sure that safety guards, controls, and emergency shut-off devices are working properly." (paraphrase)
- "Close and latch the hood."
  - guidance: SHIB: "ensure that the chipper's disc hood is completely closed and latched" (paraphrase)
- "Aim the discharge chute so chips can't hit anyone."
  - guidance: SHIB: "Ensure that the discharge chute is positioned to prevent chips from hitting employees." (paraphrase)
- "Stow all ropes and lines away from the chipper."
  - guidance: OSHA 3940: "Stow all ropes and lines away from the chipper." (exact)
- "Know your feed control bar. Pushed to the center, it stops the feed rollers."
  - guidance: SHIB: "Pushing the feed control bar to the center (neutral position) stops the feed rollers." (paraphrase)

## Feeding it
- "Stand to the side of the infeed chute."
  - guidance: SHIB: "Stand to the side of the infeed chute when feeding material into the chipper." OSHA 3940: "Stand to the side when feeding materials." (paraphrase)
- "Never stand in front of the feed table while it's running, and never stand, sit, or climb on the chipper."
  - guidance: SHIB: "Do not stand in front of the feed table when the chipper is running." "Never stand, sit or climb onto any part of the chipper while it is running." (paraphrase)
  - Reviewer note: the second clause drops "while it is running" because the sentence already sets that context.
- "Keep your hands and feet out of the infeed chute area. Push material in with a wooden tool or a long branch."
  - guidance: SHIB: "Keep hands and feet out of the immediate infeed chute area while the chipper is running." "Push material into feed rollers with a wooden tool or a long branch." (paraphrase)
- "Feed the cut end first. Lay short branches on top of long ones, and put small debris in a trash can."
  - guidance: OSHA 3940: "Feed the cut end in first, and use larger pieces to feed in small branches." SHIB: "Feed branches into the chipper butt-end first." "Place shorter branches on top of longer branches being fed into the chipper." "Place small debris into trash cans instead of feeding it into the chipper." (paraphrase)
- "Only wood goes in. Never rocks or metal."
  - guidance: OSHA 3940: "Only feed wood material into the machine, never rocks or metal." (paraphrase)

## Dress for it
- "Wear eye and face protection, a hard hat, and hearing protection."
  - guidance: SHIB: "Use appropriate eye and face protection" "Wear a protective helmet." OSHA 3940: "Wear safety glasses, a hard hat and hearing protection." /landscaping/hazards: "Wear ear protection for chipping/chainsaw." (paraphrase)
- "Chippers make hazardous noise, and material can get thrown out."
  - guidance: OSHA 3940: "Chippers also produce hazardous noise levels." "Workers can be injured by material thrown from the machine." (paraphrase)
- "Wear close-fitting, tucked-in clothing with no loose straps, gloves without cuffs, and no jewelry."
  - guidance: SHIB: "Wear close-fitting and tucked-in clothing with no stray straps or strings." Gloves "with no cuffs (non-gauntlet)." "Jewelry should not be worn while operating chippers". OSHA 3940: "Wear close-fitting clothing, gloves without cuffs, and skid-resistant shoes." (paraphrase)

## Jams and walking away
- "Never reach into the infeed chute to clear a jam while the chipper is running."
  - guidance: OSHA 3940: "Never reach into the chipper's in-feed chute to un-jam materials while it is operating." (paraphrase)
- "Before any servicing, shut it down and lock it out."
  - guidance: SHIB: "Before beginning any servicing operation, lockout/tagout procedures must be utilized to control hazardous energy" (paraphrase)
  - 1910.147(d)(2): "The machine or equipment shall be turned off or shut down using the procedures established for the machine or equipment." (d)(4)(i): "Lockout or tagout devices shall be affixed to each energy isolating device by authorized employees." (paraphrase)
- "Don't open an access cover until the disc or drum comes to a complete stop, and use the locking pins to hold the disc still."
  - guidance: SHIB: "Chipper access covers or doors must not be opened until the drum or disc comes to a complete stop" "Use proper locking pins to immobilize the disc cutting wheel when attempting to clear a clogged chipper chute." (paraphrase)
  - Related: 1910.147(d)(5)(i): "all potentially hazardous stored or residual energy shall be relieved, disconnected, restrained" (a coasting disc is stored energy; reviewer may treat this link as interpretation).
- "Leaving it unattended? Shut it down and take the key."
  - guidance: SHIB: "Shut down the chipper and remove the ignition key when it is unattended." (paraphrase)

## Ask

  - guidance: SHIB: "Designate one or more employees as a safety watch to be stationed near emergency shut-off devices" (supports the safety-watch question).
