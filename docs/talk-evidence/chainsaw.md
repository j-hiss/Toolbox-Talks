# Evidence: chainsaw

Fetched 2026-10-07 from osha.gov: 1910.266; OSHA Fact Sheet FS-3920 "Working Safely with Chainsaws" (CHAINSAWS.pdf); OSHA QuickCard 3269 "Chainsaw Safety" (CHAIN_SAW_SAFETY.pdf); OSHA Landscaping Hazards and Solutions page (/landscaping/hazards).

Scope note for reviewer: 1910.266 is the logging standard. (b)(1): "This standard establishes safety practices, means, methods and operations for all types of logging"; (c) defines logging operations as "Operations associated with felling and moving trees and logs from the stump to the point of delivery." It does not cover general landscaping or farm chainsaw use as such. There is no general-industry or agriculture (1928) chainsaw standard outside logging. So the talk is built from OSHA's chainsaw fact sheet and QuickCard (guidance), and only the leg-protection line names the logging rule explicitly. Where a 1910.266(e)(2) paragraph matches the guidance it's listed as backing, not as a rule that applies to landscaping or agriculture.

## Hook
- "Before anyone runs a saw today, let's cover four things: the gear you wear, how you start it, how to avoid kickback, and how you move with it." Agenda only; no claim.

## Gear up
- "Wear protection for your head, eyes, face, ears, hands, feet, and legs every time you run the saw."
  - guidance: QuickCard: "Proper personal protective equipment must be worn when operating the saw, which includes hand, foot, leg, eye, face, hearing and head protection." (paraphrase)
- "No loose clothing."
  - guidance: QuickCard: "Do not wear loose-fitting clothing." (paraphrase)
- "Your company has to provide that gear and make sure you use it."
  - guidance: FS-3920: "Employers must provide and ensure workers properly use PPE, including:" (paraphrase)
- "For your legs, wear chaps."
  - guidance: /landscaping/hazards (Tree Care, Solutions): "Train safe chainsaw use and wear chaps." (paraphrase)
- "In logging work, the rule says leg protection must be cut-resistant, like ballistic nylon, and the company pays for it."
  - 1910.266(d)(1)(iv): "The employer shall provide, at no cost to the employee, and assure that each employee who operates a chain saw wears leg protection constructed with cut-resistant material, such as ballistic nylon." (paraphrase; scope stated as logging)

## Before you start it
- "Check the controls, chain tension, bolts, and handles. Make sure every safety device works."
  - guidance: FS-3920: "Check controls, chain tension and all bolts and handles to ensure they are functioning properly and adjusted according to the manufacturer's instructions." "Check that all safety devices are working properly." (paraphrase)
- "If the saw is damaged or a safety device is off, don't run it."
  - guidance: FS-3920: "Do not operate a chainsaw that is damaged or has disengaged safety devices." (paraphrase)
- "Fuel at least 10 feet away from anything that could ignite it."
  - guidance: FS-3920: "Fuel the saw at least 10 feet away from ignition sources." (paraphrase). Logging backing: 1910.266(e)(2)(iv) (10 feet from open flame or ignition source; summary only).
- "Start it at least 10 feet from where you fueled, on the ground or something solid, with the chain brake on."
  - 1910.266(e)(2)(v): "The chain saw shall be started at least 10 feet (3 m) from the fueling area." (logging)
  - 1910.266(e)(2)(vi): "The chain saw shall be started on the ground or where otherwise firmly supported." (logging)
  - 1910.266(e)(2)(vii): "The chain saw shall be started with the chain brake engaged." (logging)
  - guidance: FS-3920: "Start the saw on the ground or another firm support with the brake engaged." (exact). The QuickCard also says to start at least 10 feet from the fueling area with the chain brake on (paraphrase of summary).
- "Never drop-start a saw."
  - guidance: QuickCard: "Drop starting is never allowed." (paraphrase); 1910.266(e)(2)(vi): "Drop starting a chain saw is prohibited."

## Kickback
- "To avoid kickback, don't cut with the tip, and keep the tip guard in place."
  - guidance: QuickCard: "To avoid kickback, do not saw with the tip." "Keep tip guard in place." (paraphrase)
- "Gas saws must have a device that cuts down kickback, like a chain brake."
  - guidance: QuickCard: "Gasoline-powered chainsaws must be equipped with a protective device that minimizes chainsaw kickback." (paraphrase). Chain brake as the example: FS-3920: "use saws that reduce kickback danger (chain brakes, low kickback chains, guide bars, etc.)."
- "Keep both hands on the handles and keep solid footing."
  - guidance: FS-3920: "Keep both hands on the handles and maintain secure footing." (paraphrase). Logging backing: 1910.266(e)(2)(viii), (ix).
- "Don't cut directly overhead."
  - guidance: FS-3920: "Do not cut directly overhead." (exact). Logging backing: 1910.266(e)(2)(xi).
- "Watch for branches under tension. They can spring out when cut, and wood can bind the saw."
  - guidance: QuickCard: "Watch for branches under tension; they may spring out when cut." "Be careful that the trunk or tree limbs will not bind against the saw." (paraphrase)

## Moving with the saw
- "Release the throttle or shut it off before you step back from a cut."
  - guidance: FS-3920: "Shut off or release throttle prior to retreating." (paraphrase). Logging backing: 1910.266(e)(2)(xiii).
- "Carrying it more than 50 feet, or over rough or slippery ground? Shut it off or set the chain brake."
  - guidance: FS-3920: "Shut off or engage the chain brake whenever the saw is carried more than 50 feet or across hazardous terrain." QuickCard: "Shut off the saw or engage its chain brake when carrying the saw on rough or uneven terrain." (paraphrase)
  - 1910.266(e)(2)(xiv) (logging): "...whenever a saw is carried further than 50 feet (15.2 m)... when a saw is carried less than 50 feet if conditions such as, but not limited to, the terrain, underbrush and slippery surfaces, may create a hazard for an employee." ("slippery" comes from here.)
- "Carry it so you can't touch the chain or the muffler."
  - 1910.266(e)(2)(xii) (logging): "The chain saw shall be carried in a manner that will prevent operator contact with the cutting chain and muffler." (paraphrase). Reviewer note: this line comes only from the logging rule; no fetched guidance page says it outright. Stated as practice, not as a rule for landscaping or agriculture.
- "And never work alone."
  - guidance: FS-3920: "Never work alone." (exact)
