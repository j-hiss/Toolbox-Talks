Scope note: Subpart J (1926.350-.354) covers gas welding and cutting and "welding, cutting, and heating"; the talk applies 1926.352/.353 to torch brazing and soldering as heating work. No OSHA standard is specific to plumbing soldering. FS-3647 is written for welding; its fume-position tips are applied to brazing fumes by analogy (reviewer may drop 3.2 if strict).

Dropped for lack of a source or scope:
- Fixed fire-watch time (30 minutes): 1910.252 only (general industry); 1926.352(e) says "sufficient period". Not stated.
- Hot work permits: not in 1926.352; not used.
- Flux skin/eye burns from contact, acid fluxes for soldering: no fetched OSHA page covers them.
- Close cylinder valve when done/empty/moved (1926.350(a)(8)): verified via sibling talk, cut for item count.
- Fuel-gas valve not opened more than 1 1/2 turns (1926.350(d)(2)): verified, omitted (applies to fuel gas cylinders with regulators; less relevant to hand torches).
- Drums/containers that held flammables (1926.352(i)): verified, omitted as off-topic for pipe work.
