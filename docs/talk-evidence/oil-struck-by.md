# oil-struck-by evidence

OSHA has no single struck-by standard for oil and gas well sites. Most lines rest on OSHA guidance (Oil and Gas
Extraction hazards page and the Oil and Gas eTool). The one standard cited is 1910.212(a)(1)-(a)(2), machine guarding.
The URL given in the assignment (https://www.osha.gov/oil-and-gas-extraction) is the topic home; the content came from
its /hazards subpage. The eTool page https://www.osha.gov/etools/oil-and-gas/general-safety/struck-by does not exist
(404); struck-by content is on .../general-safety/common-wellsite-incidents.
