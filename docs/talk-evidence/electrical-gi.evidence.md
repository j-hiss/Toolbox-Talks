Corrections and notes:
- "Damaged cords" and cord handling come from 1910.334 (Use of equipment), not 1910.305(g). 1910.305(g) covers permitted/prohibited uses, splices and strain relief. I added 1910.334 as a source.
- "Keep panels clear 3 ft": the storage ban is 1910.303(g)(1)(ii); depth is (g)(1)(i)(A) with Table S-1; width 30 in. is (g)(1)(i)(B). 3 ft is the minimum for most 600 V-or-less equipment, not a flat rule: 3.5 or 4 ft applies at 151-600 V under Conditions B/C, and 2.5 ft is allowed for pre-April 16, 1981 installations.
- The osha.gov rendering of Table S-1 is garbled (footnote superscripts merge into the numbers, showing "10.9 / 13.0"). Values were verified on eCFR: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/subtitle-B/chapter-XVII/part-1910/subpart-S/subject-group-ECFR1d8a2a53a5fcbfa/section-1910.303. Worth a human look at the osha.gov page before citing it in the app.

Dropped / not used:
- Overhead line distances (1910.333(c)(3)), conductive apparel (c)(8), nonconductive ladders (c)(7): accurate but out of scope for this talk.
- Cord splice rules (1910.305(g)(2)(ii)): too detailed for spoken talk.
