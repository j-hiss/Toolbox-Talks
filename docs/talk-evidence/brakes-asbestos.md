Paragraph corrections:
- Compressed-air limit is 1910.1001(f)(1)(ix) and (k)(3); Appendix F itself has no compressed-air ban (it permits compressed air inside the enclosure, [A](4)).
- 1910.1001(a) does not mention brake/clutch work; the brake rule is (f)(3)(i).

Dropped:
- PEL 0.1 f/cc and excursion 1.0 f/cc (1001(c)) — verified but left out; not needed for the crew-level message.
- Equivalent-method details ([C], 0.016 f/cc) — office-level, left out.
- "Laundered at a facility equipped for asbestos" — SHIB text came back summarized only; replaced by AppF [D](2) wording.
- "No dry rags" as a separate rule — only EPA advice, not an OSHA rule; covered implicitly by the wet method.
- Shop vacs without HEPA (EPA home-mechanic advice) — EPA aimed it at home mechanics; left out.
- Training (1001(j)(7)) — verified, left out for length.
