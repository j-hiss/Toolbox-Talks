# extinguishers evidence

Scope notes: 1910.157 applies to extinguishers "provided for the use of employees" ((a)). Paragraph (d) (travel distances) does not apply to extinguishers outside buildings ((a)), and employers with a total-evacuation policy or designated-users plan are exempt from (d) ((b)(1)-(b)(2)); hence "generally" on the distance line. 1926.150 covers construction.
