**Overlap check.** The existing talk `aerial-lift` is construction-only (1926.453, includes scissor lifts under 1926.451/454). This is the general-industry version under 1910.67 for landscaping/tree care, facilities and utilities. 1910.67 covers vehicle-mounted aerial devices only; scissor lifts are not covered here and are not mentioned. Wind is limited to the fact sheet's "high wind" work-zone item (no mph figure).

Dropped / not used:
- Wind speed limit in mph: no figure in FS or 1910.67; only "Do not operate an aerial lift in high winds above those recommended by the manufacturer." Not stated as a number.
- 1910.67(c)(2)(v) originally said "body belt"; current text (per eCFR) is personal fall arrest or travel restraint meeting subpart I. Talk uses the current wording.
- 1910.269(g)(2)(iv)(C)(1) (aerial lift fall protection for electric power work): verified, but scope is 1910.269 work; left out to keep one rule set.

## Checker note (2026-10-08)

New 4.2 from 1910.67(b)(4).
