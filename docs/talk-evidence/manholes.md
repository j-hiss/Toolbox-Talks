# manholes — evidence

Pages fetched 2026-10-07 with WebFetch: osha.gov 1910.268 ((o)), 1910.269 page 1 ((e)), 1910.269 page 2 (`1910.269_2`, (t)), OSHA Confined Spaces topic page.

Scope: 1910.268 covers telecommunications work; 1910.269(e) and (t) cover electric power generation, transmission and distribution. Neither covers, e.g., sewer manholes for plumbing crews; the talk says so and points to confined space rules (OSHA lists manholes as a confined space example). The confined space standards themselves (1910.146, 1926 Subpart AA) were not used as sources.

Paragraph corrections: in 1910.268(o), (o)(1) is guarding plus first-aid person, (o)(2) is testing/ventilation before entry, (o)(3) is joint power/telecom manholes, (o)(4) is ladders, (o)(5) is flames.
