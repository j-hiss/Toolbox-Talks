# welding-con evidence

All standards fetched from https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/<section> on 2026-10-07.

| English sentence | Source | Supporting quote |
|---|---|---|
| Welding and cutting bring fire, gas under pressure, and blinding light to the job all at once. | 1926.352 (fire), 1926.350 (compressed gas cylinders), 1926.102(a)(1) | paraphrase: 1926.102(a)(1) lists "potentially injurious light radiation" |
| Get the setup right before anyone lights a torch or strikes an arc. | n/a | Framing; no rule claimed. |
| Cylinders stay upright and secured, chained or in a cart, so they can't get knocked over. | 1926.350(a)(9), (a)(7) | exact: "Compressed gas cylinders shall be secured in an upright position at all times"; (a)(7) paraphrase: cylinder truck, chain, or other steadying device to "keep cylinders from being knocked over while in use." |
| The only exception is a short time while one is being hoisted or carried. | 1926.350(a)(9) | paraphrase: "except, if necessary, for short periods of time while cylinders are actually being hoisted or carried" |
| When a cylinder is moved or stored, the valve cap goes on and gets secured. | 1926.350(a)(1) (under "Transporting, moving, and storing compressed gas cylinders") | exact: "Valve protection caps shall be in place and secured." |
| Close the valve when the job is done, when it's empty, and any time it's moved. | 1926.350(a)(8) | paraphrase: valve closed when work is finished, when cylinders are empty, or are moved; exact fragment "the cylinder valve shall be closed." |
| In storage, keep oxygen at least 20 feet from fuel gas and anything that burns. | 1926.350(a)(10) | exact: "a minimum distance of 20 feet (6.1 m)" (oxygen cylinders in storage, from fuel-gas cylinders or combustible materials) |
| Or put a noncombustible wall at least 5 feet high between them, with at least a half-hour fire rating. | 1926.350(a)(10) | exact: "a noncombustible barrier at least 5 feet (1.5 m) high"; "a fire-resistance rating of at least one-half hour." |
| Keep cylinders back from the work, or behind fire-resistant shields, so sparks and slag can't reach them. | 1926.350(b)(1) | exact: "so that sparks, hot slag, or flame will not reach them."; paraphrase: fire-resistant shields where this is impractical |
| Never take them into a confined space. | 1926.350(b)(4) | exact: "Cylinders containing oxygen or acetylene or other fuel gas shall not be taken into confined spaces." |
| A damaged cylinder doesn't get used. | 1926.350(c)(3) | exact: "No damaged or defective cylinder shall be used." |
| Inspect every gas hose at the start of each shift. | 1926.350(f)(3) | exact: "shall be inspected at the beginning of each working shift." |
| A hose that's damaged, or one you're not sure about, doesn't get used. | 1926.350(f)(4) | exact: "Defective hose, or hose in doubtful condition, shall not be used." |
| Keep hoses and cables out of walkways, ladders and stairs. | 1926.350(f)(7) | exact: "Hoses, cables, and other equipment shall be kept clear of passageways, ladders and stairs." |
| Keep oil and grease away from oxygen cylinders and fittings. | 1926.350(i) | exact: "Oxygen cylinders and fittings shall be kept away from oil or grease." |
| Stepping away from an arc welder? Pull the electrode and set the holder where it can't touch anyone or anything that conducts. | 1926.351(d)(1) | exact: "When electrode holders are to be left unattended, the electrodes shall be removed" ... "cannot make electrical contact with employees" ... "or conducting objects." |
| Report bad equipment to your supervisor. | 1926.351(d)(4) | exact: "Any faulty or defective equipment shall be reported to the supervisor." |
| If the piece can be moved to a safe spot, move it. | 1926.352(a) | exact: "When practical, objects to be welded, cut, or heated shall be moved to a designated safe location" |
| If not, move anything nearby that can burn, or protect it. | 1926.352(a) | paraphrase: all movable fire hazards in the vicinity taken to a safe place, or otherwise protected |
| No welding or cutting where flammable paint, other flammable material, or heavy dust creates a hazard. | 1926.352(c) | exact: "No welding, cutting, or heating shall be done where the application of flammable paints"; paraphrase: "or the presence of other flammable compounds, or heavy dust concentrations creates a hazard" |
| A working fire extinguisher has to be right there in the work area. | 1926.352(d) | exact: "Suitable fire extinguishing equipment shall be immediately available in the work area" (and "maintained in a state of readiness for instant use") |
| On a wall, floor or ceiling, sparks and heat can go through, so protect the other side too. | 1926.352(f) | exact: "since direct penetration of sparks or heat transfer may introduce a fire hazard to an adjacent area"; paraphrase: same precautions on the opposite side |
| When normal precautions aren't enough, someone is assigned as fire watch, during the work and long enough afterward to be sure there's no fire. | 1926.352(e) | exact: "When the welding, cutting, or heating operation is such that normal fire prevention precautions are not sufficient"; paraphrase: additional personnel assigned to guard against fire during the work and for a sufficient period after completion |
| Anyone welding, cutting or heating wears eye protection made for it. | 1926.353(e)(2) | exact: "Employees performing any type of welding, cutting, or heating shall be protected by suitable eye protective equipment" |
| OSHA's shade chart is the guide for picking the filter lens. | 1926.102(c)(1) | exact: "Table E-1 shall be used as a guide for the selection of the proper shade numbers." |
| A darker shade is fine if you need it. | 1926.102(c)(1), Table E-1 note | exact: "Shades more dense than those listed may be used to suit the individual's needs." |
| Where practicable, screen off arc welding so the rays don't hit people working nearby. | 1926.351(e) | exact: "Whenever practicable, all arc welding and cutting operations shall be shielded by noncombustible or flameproof screens" ... "which will protect employees and other persons working in the vicinity from the direct rays of the arc." |
| In a confined space, ventilation is required. | 1926.353(b)(1) | exact: "shall be provided whenever welding, cutting, or heating is performed in a confined space." |
| With a fume exhaust, put the hood as close to the work as you can. | 1926.353(a)(3) | exact: "intended to be placed by the welder or burner as close as practicable to the work." |
| Galvanized, lead or cadmium metal needs ventilation, respirators, or both. | 1926.353(c)(1)-(c)(2) | paraphrase: (c)(1) zinc-bearing/zinc-coated, lead base, cadmium-bearing metals in enclosed spaces need mechanical ventilation; (c)(2) lead and cadmium need local exhaust or airline respirators; beryllium both. exact fragments: "Zinc-bearing base or filler metals or metals coated with zinc-bearing materials;" "Lead base metals;" |
| Even outdoors, it takes a filter-type respirator. | 1926.353(c)(3) | exact: "Employees performing such operations in the open air shall be protected by filter-type respirators" (beryllium: airline respirators) |
| Ask before you start. | n/a | Instruction. |

Dropped / notes:
- 1926.354 (preservative coatings) not used; keeping the talk within 450 words. Lead/cadmium coverage comes from 1926.353(c).
- Specific Table E-1 shade numbers left out: Table E-1 is a guide ("shall be used as a guide"), and listing numbers by electrode size would overload a spoken talk.
- "Never use oxygen in place of compressed air": not found in 1926.350 as fetched; left out.
- Fire watch duration: 1926.352(e) sets no minutes; none stated.
- Hose color/feel rule 1926.350(f)(1) cut for length.
- Paragraph corrections: in 1926.353, "as close as practicable" is (a)(3), not (a)(1)/(a)(2); inert-gas metal-arc welding is (d), and general welding/eye protection is (e). The shade table is in 1926.102(c)(1), not (b).
