Notes for reviewer:
- No OSHA construction standard addresses roofing kettles by name. 1926.150, 1926.151 and 1926.153 were checked: none mention kettles, tar, asphalt or roofing. Kettle-specific content comes from OSHA 3755 and NIOSH 2003-107 (guidance).
- OSHA 3755 says "hot tar"; NIOSH 2003-107 says "hot asphalt". The talk uses each term where its source does.
- "OSHA standards do not specifically address asphalt fumes" (OSHA asphalt-fumes page, exact).
